Inscription explorer

Find one inscription and walk its evidence trail (protocol step 3), keeping observation, transcription, hypothesis, and claim visibly separate (protocol step 4). The four kinds are four labelled layers on this screen, never blended into one text.

Find (left rail)

  • Corpus picker listing only identity-verified corpora, each with its census and licensing note; a corpus whose identity fails is listed as unavailable, with the reason.
  • Filters: site, object class, sign present, length. Result list shows catalogue identity and context, not a reading.
sources: corpus identity registry (8 identities); canonical inscription store; site-attributed corpus layer

Interactions

  • Selecting a sign in the sequence highlights every layer's statement about that sign, and only that sign.
  • "Evidence trail" walks claim → evidence → source in the evidence inspection surface, pre-filtered to this inscription.

This surface refuses to

  • Present a transcription as the inscription itself, or merge transcription layers into one silent consensus text.
  • Normalize, complete, or re-order a damaged sequence without the decision being shown as a transcription act.
  • Show a sign's proposed reading inside the observation layer.
  • Offer a corpus whose identity verification fails, under any label.
