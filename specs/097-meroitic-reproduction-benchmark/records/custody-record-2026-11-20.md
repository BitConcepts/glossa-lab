# Meroitic Benchmark — Custody Record: the Step-0 Ruling Restated as Owner Risk Acceptance (Spec 097)

**Date: 2026-11-20. This record corrects the character of
the step-0 licensing ruling, following the whole-program
red team of 2026-11-20 (finding F-03). The step-0 identity
record (`records/meroitic-benchmark-step0-identity-record-2026-11-20.md`)
is NOT edited; its §3 stands as the historical statement of
what was ruled at the time. This record states what that
ruling is, in law-shaped terms, from now on.**

## 1. The ruling renamed

The step-0 record ruled, by the program's own act, that the
absence of a license on the Otten corpus repository
permitted "scholarly reproduction only, in held custody,"
and on that basis recorded the scoping falsifier's step-0
arm (NOT EXECUTABLE AS SCOPED if the corpus is
unlicensable) as not fired.

That ruling is renamed on the record for what it is: **a
risk acceptance by the owner**, not a licensing finding.
The program is not the rights holder and has no standing to
convert the absence of a license into a permission. Default
copyright applies to the authors' compilation and code, as
the step-0 record itself states.

- **Accepting party: the owner, Tristen Pierson**, by the
  delegation of 2026-11-20 (the Glossa-Lab delegated-decisions
  package, Item 2), which adopted the red team's recommended
  change on this point.
- **Risk accepted:** that the authors or another rights
  holder could object to the held-custody reproduction use
  described in §2, with reputational and, in the limit,
  legal consequence for the program's benchmark and campaign
  records. If an objection arrives, the custody ends and the
  affected records are annotated; nothing in this record
  argues otherwise.
- **The falsifier's step-0 arm** is therefore carried from
  now on as: *not fired, on the basis of an owner risk
  acceptance, not on the basis of a licensing finding.* The
  stricter reading — that any use without a license is a
  bar — remains stated in the step-0 record, and any reader
  who holds it has the full evidence there.
