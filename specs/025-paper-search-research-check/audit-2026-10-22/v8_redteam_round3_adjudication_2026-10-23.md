## D. What the sustained set means

**The paper's science did not move in round 3; its surface did, in three specific places.** No sustained item overturns a number the paper's argument rests on. The headline arithmetic (C.1, C.2, C.11, C.12), the positional statistic, the fish count, the KL ordering, the Tamil-Brahmi ratio and the SA bound all survived a third hostile pass, and the 36 positives — every one the attacker offered — are confirmed. What failed is narrower and more useful:

1. **Two printed tables were not drawn from the records they cite.** §3.4's M267 counts came from a basis note rather than the Phase-132 test whose statistic the same paragraph prints (R3X-1), and §3.8's "strongest associations" omitted ranks 4–6 of the very ranking it reproduces (R3X-2). Neither error changes a conclusion — M267 is motif-independent on either table, and the enrichment result is stronger on the corrected table — but both are exactly the kind of error a reader *can* catch, and catching one teaches a reader to distrust the numbers they cannot catch. These two items are the round's most consequential, and both are cured by printing the record.
2. **The round-2 cures still have not reached every echo.** §4.4 twice (R3-06, R3-32), §3.27 (R3-29), §3.21's vocabulary (R3-26), Appendix C twice (R3-12, R3-34), §3.12's one surviving phrase (R3-36), §3.24's closing sentence (R3-28), §5's lead clause (R3-11), and §3.19's final sentence (R3X-3) all state, at pre-cure strength, things the body of the same paper has already withdrawn or qualified. This is the same failure mode as round 2, in fewer places. After this round's replacements are applied, a mechanical sweep — searching the .tex for each cured phrase's pre-cure form — is the obvious safeguard, and the revision worker should run one.
3. **The remaining CRITICAL-labelled attacks are not defects but the programme's open frontier, and the paper now says so at every point the attacker pressed.** The uncalibrated labels (R3-07), the unrecorded DEDR search (R3-04), the unreleased corpus (R3-01), the untested rival templates and language families (R3-08, R3-12), and the unrecomputed SA/V07-free headline (R3-05, R3-51) are each disclosed in the paper at the strength the records support. An adjudication can cure wording; it cannot manufacture calibration, a search log, a corpus release, or a rival-model run. Those five items are the owner's research decisions, collected here so they are decided rather than drifted past: (i) release the Holdat corpus or an inscription-level derived table; (ii) commission a calibration of the HIGH/MEDIUM labels (blind known-script test or expert-coded sample); (iii) re-base the headline without SA and with the V07 drivers addressed, or formally decline to; (iv) run the rival-template and rival-family comparisons §4.4 names; (v) archive the exact v8 source and supplements under the Zenodo DOI. Until they are decided, the honest description of the paper — which its text, after this round's cures, now gives — is an exploratory computational model with verified internal arithmetic and uncalibrated readings, not a validated decipherment.

**What this does not establish.** This adjudication establishes that the round-2-revised draft's numbers check against the programme's own records, except where §A/§C say otherwise, and that its wording, after §B's replacements, matches the strength of those records. It does not establish that the records themselves are right about the corpus, that the readings are correct, or that the paper is ready to submit: the standing gate (owner's read of the revised build; TeX production; this red-team pass being the third) is a matter for the owner, and corpus-level truth cannot be adjudicated from inside the programme's own files.
