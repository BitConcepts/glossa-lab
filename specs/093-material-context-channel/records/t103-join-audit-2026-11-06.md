## 5. The join, its coverage bound, and the material arm

The join output is the 179 K2 pairs: for each, the snapshot
text record's identity plus the CSV row's data-list fields
(material raw, site, period, type, area-section,
excavation-idno, dimensions). **Coverage: 179 of the 3,085
text-bearing records (5.8%), one site (Mohenjo-daro).** The
Mahadevan population (2,906 records) has no in-repo
concordance coverage — stated as the freeze states it, not
repaired. The museum-accession route (K1) failed its floor
and contributes nothing.

**The floor was cleared, so the material arm is NOT
stopped**: Stage 4's material pass may be administered over
this join's population when its own stage comes, gated
there by its own freeze and by C093-1's standing (Stage 2).
Two bounds ride with the join: (1) the delivered material
values are the CSV's RAW values (23 distinct) — the T102
register is bound to the snapshot's values, so normalizing
the joined values needs a successor register version or
amendment first; (2) the join establishes pair identity to
the frozen criterion, not that the delivered fields describe
the objects accurately — the content disagreements in §4(b)
are censused, not adjudicated away.

*This does not establish:* that the join is correct pair by
pair — precision on the sample is the claim, and a join
below its floor stops the material arm honestly (C093-8).
(T103's line, carried verbatim; as executed, the audit was
a complete enumeration rather than a sample, and the
measured precision is §3's.)
