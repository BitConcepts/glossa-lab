# Spec 094 — Claims

Every claim carries at least one falsifier (a concrete observation that
would show it false) and a "does not establish" line (the over-reading it
forbids), per the program's claims discipline. The acceptance claims
carry probe-shaped falsifiers: each names the adversarial act — drawn
from the 2026-11-13 acceptance-path probe battery — and the exact
observation that would break the claim. The same set, normalized into
`aee-claims.json` without substantive change, is validated under the
pinned AEE (1.5.3) at adoption (Stage 0;
`records/adoption-aee-assessment.json`). Grounding for every machinery
statement below: `records/commission-and-grounding-2026-11-13.md`, from
the acceptance-path test of 2026-11-13 (code survey and adversarial
probes on a scratch backend at main `25bcbe9a`; the live backend was
never written to).

## Approval gate (Stage 1)

**C094-1 — Approval requires a falsifier and a named human.** No
candidate reaches `approved` unless its record carries a non-empty
falsifier statement and an approver identity that validates as a named
human under the `claim_stage` actor rules (reused, not redefined).
*Falsifier:* exhibit, on the built system, one approved candidate whose
record lacks either field, reached through any endpoint or lifecycle
route.
*Does not establish:* that the falsifier is a good one — the gate binds
presence and attribution, not quality.

**C094-2 — Automated actors can never approve, verify, or promote.** No
identity classified as automated under the `claim_stage` rules
(allowlist or process-token) can effect an approve, verify, or promote
transition on the anchor path; the loop pipeline's write authority ends
at creating and staging candidates.
*Falsifier:* drive any automated identity through the anchor-path
endpoints and produce an approved, verified, or promoted state change
attributable to it.
*Does not establish:* anything about automated staging, which stays
permitted and is the loop's job.

**C094-3 — Gate inputs live on the record, not the request.** The
falsifier, approver, and evidence fields the gates read are first-class
fields of the candidate record; no gate decision is taken from data
supplied only in the request that asks for the decision.
*Falsifier:* effect one approval or promotion whose deciding input
exists in the request but nowhere on the candidate record beforehand.
*Does not establish:* a schema for its own sake — the claim binds where
gate inputs live, because defect 2's request-supplied ref is the
failure it closes.

## Promotion evidence (Stage 2)

**C094-4 — Promotion evidence is a pinned artifact.** Promotion
requires, per candidate, an evidence artifact pinned by content hash
(SHA-256 recorded on the record at pin time) whose bytes verify against
the pin at promotion time. A bare path, a `path:KEY` string, or an
unpinned request-supplied reference does not promote.
*Falsifier:* promote any candidate whose recorded evidence pin is
absent, does not match the artifact's bytes, or was created by the
promote request itself.
*Does not establish:* that a verified pin makes the evidence relevant —
that is C094-5's separate bar.

**C094-5 — Evidence must be of a registered class, and the loop cannot
evidence itself.** The pinned artifact must belong to a named evidence
class on a registered list (external corpus record, published
concordance entry, independent-expert record, adjudicated claim dossier
— the list as ratified at Stage 2). Artifacts generated by the loop or
the anchor path about the candidate itself — pipeline outputs, staging
and archive files, run syntheses — are not a qualifying class.
*Falsifier:* promote a candidate on evidence whose class is loop output
about that candidate, or a class absent from the registered list.
*Does not establish:* the truth of any class member — classes govern
admissibility, not correctness.

## Verification (Stage 3)

**C094-6 — "Verified" is per-candidate or it is nothing.** A candidate
reaches `verified` only through a recorded evaluation of that
candidate, citing the per-candidate evidence it rests on. No run-level
aggregate — a foundation check, a suite result, a run's overall delta —
confers the status.
*Falsifier:* exhibit a verified candidate whose record carries no
per-candidate evaluation, or whose verification cites only a run-level
signal.
*Does not establish:* a particular evaluation method; the claim binds
the granularity and the record, Stage 3 fixes the method.

**C094-7 — The flat-delta auto-verification mechanism is gone.** No
code path flips `approved` → `verified` from a foundation-check outcome
or any equivalent global signal; the `sa_delta = 0.1` construction in
`_run_anchor_lifecycle` is removed, not tuned.
*Falsifier:* run the anchor lifecycle over an approved candidate
lacking a per-candidate evaluation, with a clean foundation check, and
observe a verified flip.
*Does not establish:* that the lifecycle's other duties (expiry of
stale rejections, archiving) change — they are untouched except where
they carried the flip.

**C094-8 — MEDIUM means verified under this spec.** Promotion confers
MEDIUM confidence only on candidates verified under C094-6; an
approved-but-unverified candidate promotes at LOW at most, whatever its
evidence score.
*Falsifier:* exhibit an anchor at MEDIUM whose source candidate's
verified status came from any route other than C094-6's per-candidate
evaluation (including the pre-094 archive stock, per C094-12).
*Does not establish:* any change to HIGH, which promotion has never
conferred.

**C094-9 — No fabricated SA delta is presented as measured.** Where no
SA comparison has run for a candidate, surfaces present the delta as
absent, never as a computed estimate. (Drafting observation, from code:
`get_staging` currently substitutes `round(score * 0.12, 3)` as a
candidate's `sa_delta` when none was recorded — response-shaping only,
not persisted, and not one of the tested defects; Stage 3 demonstrates
the built behaviour rather than assuming it.)
*Falsifier:* exhibit a surface response carrying an `sa_delta` value
for a candidate with no recorded SA comparison behind it.
*Does not establish:* anything about real SA deltas where a comparison
did run.

## Routing and stores (Stage 4)

**C094-10 — Anchor-path transitions route through the gate; GR-088-05
closes for this path.** Every status transition on anchor-path records
(staged, approved, verified, promoted, rejected, archived, expired) is
effected through a single gated transition service carrying
`transition_stage`'s guarantees — one-step moves, actor validation with
the automated ceiling, an evidence trail — and no endpoint or lifecycle
code writes `review_status` directly.
*Falsifier:* find, in the built system, any code path that changes an
anchor-path record's status without passing the service, or effect one.
*Does not establish:* storage enforcement for the claim stores
generally — the closure is path-scoped and the register entry is
amended to say exactly that (amend, never delete).

**C094-11 — The anchor-path stores are written atomically.** The three
stores — `outputs/anchor_staging.json`,
`outputs/anchor_staging_archive.json`,
`backend/reports/INDUS_FINAL_ANCHORS.json` — are written only through
the platform's atomic write path (`atomic_io.atomic_write_text`, or the
verified-write successor Spec 092 Stage 2 lands, whichever is current
at build); no direct truncating write to them remains in the anchor
path, and a failed write leaves the previous file byte-identical under
a named error.
*Falsifier:* a write to any of the three stores that bypasses the
atomic path, or a simulated write failure that leaves one truncated or
zeroed.
*Does not establish:* read-back hash verification beyond the atomic
path's own guarantees — that stronger contract is Spec 092's, adopted
here only if it has landed by build time.

## Boundary and standing protections

**C094-12 — Nothing already standing is moved or grandfathered.** This
spec's diff alters no existing anchor's reading or confidence, no
claim's stage or status, and no ledger entry. Pre-gate
`approved`/`verified` archive entries keep their recorded status as
history but carry no presumption of having passed these gates: they are
inventoried by count and state in Stage 0's record, and they cannot
promote until they carry the record fields C094-1..5 require.
*Falsifier:* a diff under this spec that changes a pre-existing anchor
entry or claim record, or a promotion of a pre-gate entry that skipped
the new record fields.
*Does not establish:* that the pre-gate stock is sound or unsound — it
is unexamined by this spec, and labelled so.

**C094-13 — The loop stays off the live stores until the gates land.**
No research-loop run writes to the live anchor-path stores before the
Stage 5 closeout record discharges C094-1..11 on their evidence.
Scratch-instance loop executions for stage evidence are the only loop
runs this spec contemplates.
*Falsifier:* a live-store write attributable to a loop run before the
closeout record exists.
*Does not establish:* a permanent bar — closeout lifts it by name, and
any later loop run is ordinary operations under the landed gates.
