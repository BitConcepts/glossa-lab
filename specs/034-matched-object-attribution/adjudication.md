## 1. The adopted design (operative substance)

Design D-20, as frozen in `spec.md`:

- **The settled input:** Phase-125's FAIL —
  DISAGREEMENT stands (16 judgeable pairs of 286;
  median TV distance 0.636931), Phase-127 showed the
  disagreement is not a sampling artefact (noise
  alone 0.083), and Phase-131 attributed 6.1% of it,
  leaving 93.9% unexplained for want of shared object
  keys (zero found; the Holdat `cisi_number` join
  prohibited as non-identity).
- **Step 0, as designed:** build the object bridge
  through the three lawful routes — the CISI
  catalogue layer (7,705 rows, Phases 120–124), the
  Phase-130 intake schema on the Mahadevan side, and
  the 44 Phase-128 dossiers — every pair as cited
  data. **The gate is fixed in advance: at least 150
  matched objects with texts of three or more signs
  in both compilations**; below it the study closes
  as TOO THIN TO ATTRIBUTE, with the bridge census
  published as the finding.
- **Hypothesis and falsifier, as designed:** on
  matched objects the profiles agree far better than
  0.637 (composition carries the disagreement); the
  falsifier is a matched-object disagreement at or
  above half the corpus-level distance (0.32).
- **Method, as designed:** Phase-125's own distance
  and count floors on the matched subset only;
  decomposition in Phase-131's own categories
  (segmentation / substitution, checked against the
  crosswalk's conflict pairs / composition residue).
- **Criteria, as designed:** at or below 0.20,
  ATTRIBUTED TO COMPOSITION; at or above 0.32,
  ATTRIBUTED TO TRANSCRIPTION; between, a split
  attribution with the decomposition table as the
  result.
- **Confounders, as designed:** bridge selection bias
  (mixes reported; the verdict carries the subset's
  description); the crosswalk as a record of
  disagreement, never ground truth (762 pairs, 383
  conflict pairs).
- **Not established, as designed:** which compilation
  is right. The settled verdict is not re-judged.
