  INITIAL signs show a start rate of at least 0.40 at

  sequences. The design substitutes nothing.
- **Step 0 fails closed** (design decision DD-30-1):
  the trained INITIAL set is reconstructed from the
  recorded lineage and frozen; the withholding is

  `data/crosswalks/`, fixed before counting. A failed

  different prediction requiring a new PRED number.

  with no crosswalk counterpart are counted and
  reported; coverage above 20% of the trained set is
  stated in the verdict's first sentence (DD-30-3).

  reported as themselves, never as verdicts.

  (DD-30-2): the per-sign table (every trained sign,
