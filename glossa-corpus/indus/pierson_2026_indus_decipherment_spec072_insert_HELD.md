We tested the fish family sign by sign in a registered follow-up study whose family, measure, and pass line were all fixed in advance: the family is the seven-member fish-sign list frozen earlier in this program, and for each sign we measured its ending-follower fraction — the share of the times it is followed by another sign in the same inscription in which that following sign is itself classed, on the same rows, as a text-ending sign — on the documented-lineage rows only of the Shakti corpus, an ICIT-lineage corpus, using ICIT sign numbers. The pass line was set before any fish sign was scored, at L = 0.765, the 95th percentile of the same fraction measured for the 86 non-fish signs that appear at least 20 times in those same rows. Four family members could be tested, and none reached the line: ICIT 220 scored 0.4056, ICIT 235 scored 0.3010, ICIT 032 scored 0.2500, and ICIT 033 scored 0.0850, so the members that passed carry none of the tested occurrences (a combined passing weight of w = 0.0000) and the family is not supported as a family on this corpus. A fifth identified member, ICIT 243, appears only 9 times in these rows, below the at-least-20-times floor for testing, and was therefore not tested; the family's two remaining members, M047 and M052, are unmeasured rather than dropped, because no ICIT number could be stated for them, so nothing could be counted for them and no other sign was counted in their place. Counting the documented rows together with the corroborated rows, instead of the documented rows alone, gives the same family result, so the result does not turn on which of those two trusted sets of rows is counted. This result does not establish what any fish sign meant or was pronounced, it says nothing about any other corpus or any other numbering, and it is not a verdict on the earlier records: Spec 048's closure and the Phase-43 record stand exactly as recorded.
