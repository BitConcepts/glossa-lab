Settings

Two kinds of settings, kept apart: the tool's (the service, its environment, its AI providers — shared by every program) and the open program's (its sources, its loop schedule, its question). Changing a program setting never touches another program.

Foundation-check gate
The gate the constitution sets before anything leaves the program (publication, external letters). Its state shows on the program home; a failing gate blocks report generation here, in the open — not silently.
Passing
What is not here: accounts and permissions. Glossa Lab is a single-operator tool — generic across programs, not across tenants. Nothing in Settings invents a multi-user system.

Changed & why
