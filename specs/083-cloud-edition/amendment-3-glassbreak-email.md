# Amendment 3 to Spec 083 — The glassbreak account's email; the setup prompt (owner, 2026-10-30)

**Status**: Adopted with the spec (Phase 0). Supplements FR-003 and
claim C083-3; nothing else changes.

## The account

The glassbreak account's seed record carries, alongside the username
**glassbreak**:

- **Email: `tristen.pierson@gmail.com`** — the account's contact
  identifier, recorded in the seed design (it is where admin-issued
  password-reset links for this account would be addressed, and the
  identity the audit log's setup entry names). Login in this edition is
  by username + password (FR-004); the email is the account's recorded
  contact identifier, set at seed time, editable later only by an admin
  act that is audit-logged like any other.

## The setup prompt at go-live

When the production deployment is live and the seed step has run, the
owner receives **the glassbreak account prompt** through the
coordinator:

- the **one-time setup code** (single-use, 24-hour expiry, exactly as
  specced in FR-003 — generated by the deployment, shown once in the
  deploying operator's terminal, and passed to the owner once), and
- the **production URL: `https://glossa.bitconcepts.tech`**.

The owner opens the setup screen there, enters the code, and **sets his
own password on that screen**. Reaffirmed, because it is the point of
the design: **no password is ever collected by the agent** — not in
chat, not in code, not in configuration, not in logs. The only residue
anywhere is the salted Argon2id hash in the identity store. What the
agent handles is the *code*, once — never the password, at all.
