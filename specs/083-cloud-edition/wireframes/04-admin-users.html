Manage opens: program memberships (grant / remove per program) · make admin / make member · disable or re-enable · issue a password-reset link · remove account. Removal keeps the person's past work attributed to their name, marked removed — history is not rewritten. Disabling ends their sessions immediately. The admin's own row offers no disable or remove: the edition cannot lock out its only admin.

Annotation — wireframe 04 · not part of the UI
Purpose
User management: the whole population of the edition on one screen, with role, status, membership, and activity visible at a glance (FR-005, FR-012).
Primary action
“+ Invite user” (people enter through invites, never through a create-user form — this screen deliberately has none).
Changed & why
New surface — the admin dashboard the owner ordered. Two rules are designed in, not bolted on: the audit lede ("every action here is written to the audit log") states FR-012 where the actions happen; and the last-admin guard (no disable/remove on the sole admin's row) prevents the one self-inflicted lockout a single-admin edition cannot recover from without a redeploy.
Spec claims
C083-5 (role enforcement — this surface is admin-only, 403 to members), C083-12 (audit), C083-11 (removed users' attribution persists, stated in the Manage note).
