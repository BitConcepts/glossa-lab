Annotation — wireframe 03 · not part of the UI
Purpose
The claims register: every governed claim in the program, with its falsifier, its machine verdict, and its human digestion stage side by side but never merged.
Primary action
“Register a claim” (registration refuses a claim with no falsifier).
Changed & why
Claims today live inside a view named for one program's subject (“Evidence Graph”, served by /indus-* endpoints), next to a Hypotheses tracker that uses a conflicting vocabulary (confirmed/refuted). Here claims are a top-level program surface with the doctrine's own vocabulary; “insufficient evidence” and “awaiting expert adjudication” are first-class verdict states, shown rather than filtered away.
