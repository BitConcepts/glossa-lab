Evidence & sources

Everything a claim can stand on: the library of sources, the citation record, and correspondence with the people behind them. A source enters with its citation and provenance, or it does not enter — uncited material cannot be attached to a claim.

Open dataset
Correspondence lives here too. Letters to and from scholars are part of the evidence record — who was asked, what they answered, what was promised. Private correspondence is never published from this screen; it is tracked, with its promises, so a commitment made in a letter cannot be forgotten by the program.
Annotation — wireframe 06 · not part of the UI
Purpose
The program's source record: library, citations, and correspondence in one place, each item carrying citation, provenance, limits, and the claims that use it.
Primary action
“Add a source” (entry requires citation + provenance).
Changed & why
Merges three scattered peers (Evidence Graph's Library tab, Citations, Correspondence) into one source record. Limits and restrictions render on the item itself (author-copy privacy, the Wells numbering non-join, the Meluhha count correction) instead of living in separate report files — the caveat travels with the source, as it does with claims.
