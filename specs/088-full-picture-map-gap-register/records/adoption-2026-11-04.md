# Adoption record — Spec 088 (2026-11-04)

**Scope:** the adoption of a framework-level system — RCPH's full-picture
program map + gap register — into Glossa-Lab as Spec 088. This record is
the adoption act for the *system*. The ratification of this spec's
*package* is a separate act (plan.md Stage 0, T003), still the owner's;
nothing in this record pre-approves the package.

## The adoption act

On 2026-11-04 the RCPH chat delivered its full-picture system to the
framework's projects on the owner's direct instruction: a generated
whole-program map (every claim and every study a node, standings carried
on the nodes, filters over the generated fields), four-kind gap scans
(missing node / missing edge / thin evidence / stale bookkeeping), and a
gap register (their GR-01..GR-18, ratified by the owner as a block, first
wave executing there). This chat assessed the teaching against Glossa's
own settlement and recorded the full assessment in the adoption record at
`files/2026-11-04-spec088-adoption-full-picture-map.md` (goal workspace).

**Disposition: ADOPTED, no conflicts**, with two binding constraints the
assessment added:

- **C1 — the gate extension rides the build.** Spec 086's verification
  gate covers the ledger, the matrix, and the claim map. The program map
  is a wider population over the same ledger; it never ships around the
  gate. The build therefore includes the gate extension (a program-map
  equivalence class with its own registered definitions) before the page
  can publish. A red gate = nothing ships.
- **C2 — prose-only standings are gap-register finds, never map data.**
  RCPH's map admits curated standings entered from ratified sources.
  Glossa's ledger doctrine (truth flows one way: ledger → matrix → map)
  forbids that shape here: the builder never reads prose into the map.
  Where a standing exists only in prose, the node classes by derivation
  or as unjudged, and the shortfall is emitted as a stale-bookkeeping
  find for the register.

The assessment also recorded Glossa's starting counts (counted first-hand,
2026-11-04): 857 ledger claims (834 bundle across 60 spec directories, 23
extracted across 7 source documents), 88 spec directories, falsifier
coverage 848/857 (the 9 nulls all in the extracted population), and the
taxonomy v0 domains the package drafts against.

## Why this motion appends to the ledger

The package's claims (C088-1..19) are requirements on a system whose
outputs derive standings from the ledger; under the program's intake
discipline they enter the ledger like every spec's claims — through the
sanctioned append path, at adoption:

`tools/ledger/import_bundle.py --spec specs/088-program-map-gap-register --date <adoption date>`

— never a hand-edit of `claims.jsonl` — followed in the same motion by
regeneration of the generated layers over the grown ledger through the
gate (plan.md, T004). Claim C088-15 bounds the whole spec's ledger diff
to exactly that append.
