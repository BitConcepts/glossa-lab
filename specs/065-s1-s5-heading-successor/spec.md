## 5. Step 1 — the heading rule (Spec 057 §5, carried)

For each searched document, the rule is Spec
057 §5's, carried verbatim in substance. No
word of it is re-opened here.

### 5.1 What counts as the heading

An occurrence of "positional analysis"
(case-insensitive) counts as **the heading**
only if it is in **heading form**: it stands
as the title of a section, chapter, appendix,
or titled part of the document — shown in the
text layer by standing alone as a line or
running head, by chapter/appendix numbering,
or by a table-of-contents entry that points
to the passage it introduces. The heading
text as it stands is recorded verbatim on
the source's row.

Occurrences that are **not** the heading, and
never anchor a passage: mentions inside
running sentences; bibliography and reference
entries; figure and table captions; and
cross-references to another document's
analysis.

### 5.2 Delimitation

The passage under a heading runs from the
heading occurrence to the next heading of
equal or higher rank in the same document, or
to the document's end. Running heads repeated
across pages are one heading, not many
passages. Where the heading is the document's
own title, the passage is the document's
findings-bearing body — its stated results,
in the document's own sections — and the
verbatim record is the passage artifact of
§5.3 cited by locator, not a reprint of the
whole document in the outcome record.

### 5.3 The locator and the verbatim record

For each passage found, the row records:

- **citation** — the §2.1 citation of the
  source;
- **locator** — the document's page and the
  extracted text layer's line range of the
  passage; the heading's page and line; and
  the document's chapter/section designation
  where the document carries one;
- **verbatim passage** — the passage text
  exactly as it stands in the pinned
  document's text layer, written to a
  per-source passage artifact in this spec's
  directory at execution, whose SHA-256 is
  recorded on the row. The only permitted
  normalisation is rejoining lines broken by
  the text layer; any other alteration makes
  the row non-compliant. Tables and figures
  the passage relies on are named in the
  locator even where their content cannot be
  carried as text.

## 9. Epistemic limits (binding on all outputs)

1. **Recovery is not verification.** No
   outcome under this spec establishes any
   positional finding at all. A recovered
   claim starts unchecked, and no output may
   present a recovered proposition as
   supported by its recovery.
2. **No credit transfers.** None of the
   credit the positional topic has earned on
   other records in this program belongs on
   these rows or on a recovered claim until
   the claim earns its own check.
3. **No invented propositions.** A recovered
   claim states what its passage states, in
   plain words, with the passage quoted
   beside it. A paraphrase that strengthens,
   narrows, or numbers a claim the passage
   did not make is a defect of the execution,
   recorded as such.
4. **The frozen records stand.** Spec 057's
   spec, freeze record, outcome record, and
   the canonical heading record with its
   evidence instances are never edited; this
   spec's products append beside them.
5. **Licence, stated per copy.** The
   acquired S-5 copy carries CC-BY-4.0 as
   stated on its Zenodo record at
   acquisition (§2.2), which supersedes the
   registry's "unclear" for that copy; any
   later publication use of its passage text
   still requires attribution as that
   licence states, recorded as its own act.
   For S-1, and for the held copies
   generally, the registry posture stands:
   license_status "unclear", verbatim
   artifacts internal, and any publication
   use requires the license verification the
   registry itself demands, as its own act.
6. **The S-1 fence.** This successor never
   contacts Andreas Fuls in any form, never
   creates an account, never uses the owner's
   credentials, and never sends an
   interlibrary-loan or any other request.
   The only S-1 inputs are a document the
   owner hands over and an intake record
   that pins it.
7. **Absence is document-scoped.** HEADING
   ABSENT FROM DOCUMENT is a finding about
   the searched document at its pinned hash
   — for S-5, a file whose edition
   relationship is established (§1.1) — and
   about no other copy or edition.

## 10. Risks and honest weaknesses

- **The prior assessments point one way.**
  Both rows' held-text structures contain no
  heading, and the Item 6 draft assesses both
  headings as likely absent from the
  documents. Those assessments have no
  standing (§1.3), but they are why this spec
  freezes the ABSENT outcome and its
  reporting with the same care as FOUND: an
  honest absence, on the document itself, is
  a resolution of the row, not a null run.
- **S-1 may never be searched under this
  spec.** Its blocker is an owner account
  act outside the program's control. The
  frozen blocked arm exists so the run is
  complete and honest either way — and so
  class B does not masquerade as class A.
- **Text-layer extraction is a copy of the
  document, one step removed.** A heading
  rendered as an image inside the PDF would
  not appear in its text layer. The frozen
  question is the one Spec 057 named — the
  document *text layer* under §5 — and the
  outcome words say exactly that; a
  text-layer absence does not claim the
  printed page was inspected visually.
- **The acquired S-5 file lives outside the
  repository.** Its pin is asserted on the
  local file at step 0 (gate 0.2); on a host
  without the file the run closes at step 0
  rather than substituting other bytes. The
  venue is therefore part of the contract,
  stated here rather than discovered at run
  time (the lesson of Spec 057 defect D-3).

## 11. Stop conditions and outcome classes

Execution ends in exactly one of:

- **CLOSED AT STEP 0 — INPUT PIN MISMATCH**
  (gates 0.1, 0.2, or 0.3's verification
  limb). No text layer searched.
- **A headline class of §7** (A or B), after
  all gates pass and both sources carry an
  outcome. Class B contains a blocked source;
  it is a result of the design as written,
  not an interruption of it.

There is no outcome class for a partial
accounting: one row and a headline is a
failed execution (§1.3), recorded as such.

## 13. What this spec is not

Not a re-opening of Spec 057, not a
positional analysis, not a check of any
recovered claim, not an acquisition act
(this spec acquires nothing — S-5 was
acquired under the Item 6 package, and S-1's
acquisition is the owner's act), and not an
authorisation to execute. It is the
document-layer search Spec 057 could not
run, designed so that its honest outcomes —
a heading found, a heading shown absent from
the document itself, and a source still
blocked on an owner act — are written down
before any text layer is searched.
