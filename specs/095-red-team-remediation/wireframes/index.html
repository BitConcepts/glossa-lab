What this system does not claim

  • The Indus script is not deciphered. No panel of this dashboard says otherwise, in any wording.
  • No reading is presented stronger than its adjudicated standing; the strongest current standing is NOT IDENTIFIABLE.
  • Counts shown are the gate-verified census of record, never a larger historical figure.

Recent adjudications

  • A strip of the most recent adjudication records (claim, outcome, date, adjudicator), each linking to the claim's evidence view. Sourced from the ledger's act chain and settling records; the strip shows acts, not summaries of acts.

This surface refuses to

  • Show any number that is not the census of record (no historical cohort sizes, no vanity metrics).
  • Present a claim, reading, or translation at a strength above its adjudicated standing.
  • Offer a "decipherment progress" meter or any equivalent framing.
