Gulf Seals Survey

Who made the Gulf-type seals, and for whom? The program exists and has data. It has no claims yet — so there is nothing to verdict, digest, or report. That is the honest state, and the home says so instead of showing empty charts.

What a claim is, here

A statement that can be wrong
Registered with its falsifier: the evidence that would prove it wrong. No falsifier, no claim — the register refuses it.
Then it is checked
Experiments and the research loop test it and return a verdict: supported, not supported, inconclusive, or cannot be administered — each stated as what it means.
Then people settle it
Digestion: created → checked → explained → accepted → digested. The last three stages are human acts, recorded by name. Machines never advance a claim past checked.
