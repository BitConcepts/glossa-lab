**S9 — §3.8's threshold logic (R5-05).** *The mathematics.* The threshold number is right: at a familywise level of 0.05 over 394 tests, the per-test level is 0.05/394 = 0.0001269, and the critical value of a chi-square distribution with one degree of freedom at that level is **14.687** (recomputed by this adjudicator), printed as ≈ 14.7. One degree of freedom is the degrees of freedom of a 2×2 independence test, (2−1)(2−1) = 1. The printed per-pair statistic is the focal cell's contribution (O−E)²/E. For a 2×2 table, all four cells have the same absolute residual |O−E| = δ, and the full Pearson statistic is χ²_full = δ² · (1/E₁₁ + 1/E₁₂ + 1/E₂₁ + 1/E₂₂), a sum of four non-negative terms of which the printed contribution (δ²/E₁₁) is one. Therefore χ²_full ≥ the printed contribution, always: **a pair passing 14.7 on the single-cell contribution necessarily passes 14.7 on the full 2×2 Pearson χ²** — verified algebraically, no exceptions. The comparison the paper makes is therefore *conservative* (it can miss pairs whose full statistic passes, never invent one), *provided* the printed number is named as a contribution and the threshold is named as a critical value of the full statistic. The single-cell contribution on its own is not distributed as χ²₁, which is why the naming matters: as printed ("chi-square, one degree of freedom per pair, Bonferroni-corrected threshold"), a reader is told the printed statistic *is* the test statistic, and that is not so. *The record — what Phase-143 actually compared:* the Phase-143 script (`backend/scripts/phase143_iconographic_formula.py`, Part A) builds, for multi-sign inscriptions whose first sign is H+M, the observed INITIAL × icon counts; iterates only over pairs attested together; skips signs heading fewer than 5 such inscriptions, icon types on fewer than 5 seals in that set, and pairs with expected count below 1; computes for each surviving pair only (O−E)²/E; sorts; and counts as "enriched" the pairs with that value above **1.0** and observed above expected. Its JSON record stores `n_pairs_tested` = 394 and `n_enriched` = 63, and its interpretation field says "63 strong enrichments (χ²>1.0)". No full 2×2 statistic, p-value, or threshold appears anywhere in the script. *What Phase-149 C2 actually compared:* the C2 script reads the Phase-143 record, hard-codes 394 and 63, computes the uncorrected expectation 394 × 0.05 = 19.7, counts **14 of the top 20** associations above 14.7 (its own `adversarial_test` field), and its `test_result` then says both "All top-20 pairs (chi2 range 4.9-158.5) survive Bonferroni" — false on its own count, and not a sentence the paper prints — and "63 enriched means ~43 genuinely significant", i.e. 63 − 19.7 ≈ 43 by subtraction. Its caveat says "the top ~40 pairs are robust; the bottom ~20 are marginal". No per-pair threshold recount exists in C2. *The counts, settled:* pairs at or above 14.7 on the printed statistic are exactly **14** (from the record's sorted top-20: 158.507, 155.274, 148.804, 118.880, 116.456, 103.029, 84.573, 75.512, 66.943, 54.368, 26.466, 18.682, 16.550, 15.031; the fifteenth value is 8.964, and the list is sorted descending, so no pair outside the top 20 can reach 14.7). The expected false-positive count at the Bonferroni level under the complete null is ≈ 0.05 (394 × 0.000127), as the paper's R4R-09 sentence already states. *The denominator (P3-10), settled:* the 394 is conditioned on attestation and on the script's four filters stated above — it is not the number of INITIAL-sign × icon combinations in the corpus, it excludes every pair that never occurs together, and its size is therefore partly a product of the data. The paper's sentence "the exact inclusion rule is not stated in the record" is true of the Phase-143 JSON and false of the script; round 3's R3-22 statement to the same effect is refined on this new evidence. *What the paper must print for the comparison to be honest* (all of it in R5R-03): the statistic's name (single-cell contribution to the pair's 2×2 Pearson chi-square); what the threshold is a critical value *of* (the full 2×2 statistic, one degree of freedom, at 0.05/394); the direction of conservatism (contribution ≤ full statistic, so passes are valid and misses are possible); the correct counts (63 enriched at > 1.0; 14 at the threshold; ~43 an estimate by subtraction, not a count); and the denominator's conditioning (attested, filter-passing pairs). **Settled: SIP — the threshold's arithmetic and the passes are sound; the printed count of passes, the printed definition of "robust", and the printed account of the denominator are not.**
