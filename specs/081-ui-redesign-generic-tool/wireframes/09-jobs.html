Jobs is a tool-level surface: the queue is shared machinery. A job always names its program, and a finished job links to the claim, experiment, or report it produced — never to a raw JSON dump as the first thing you see (the structured result is one click deeper, for the record).

Purpose
All running and finished work in one queue, with honest states and timestamps.
Primary action
“New job.” Row actions (open, retry) are secondary and local to their row.
Changed & why
Jobs exist twice today (a full view and a bottom-panel mini list) and results default to raw JSON. Here there is one queue, tool-level, with the program named on every row; results render as what they mean (a verdict, a table) with the raw record one click deeper; and the failed row is full-width, explained, and actionable — the direct answer to “failures and fallthroughs” being invisible. The bottom panel's duplicate Jobs tab goes away.
