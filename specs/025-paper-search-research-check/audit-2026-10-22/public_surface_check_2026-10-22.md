# Public-surface check — BitConcepts research pages vs the audit corrections (2026-10-22)

Fix commission WS5 (prepare only — nothing was published, pushed, or
edited under this commission). Both pages were fetched as text on
2026-10-22: `https://bitconcepts.tech/research/` and
`https://bitconcepts.tech/research/indus-script/`.

## Result

**None of the four audit-corrected misstatements appears on either
page.** Three topics are entirely absent (Farmer–Sproat–Witzel framing;
Nair 2026; Parpola 2010's venue — no mention of any of them in the
fetched text). One item needs a clarifying note, not a retraction:

### Item 1 — positional structure (clarifying note recommended)

The Indus page presents the Initial / 3-slot / Terminal pattern inside
its "Registered predictions" section (e.g. "The 3-slot template holds
in new material / 3-slot template ≥ 70% in a newly acquired multi-site
dataset") with no statement of priority or novelty — but also no
attribution, so a reader could take the structure to be the program's
own. The audit established the three-part structure is published prior
art. Draft note for the owner's approval, to sit in the Registered
predictions section directly above PRED-2026-001 (or under the
PRED-2026-003 heading):

> The three-part pattern used in these predictions — signs that tend
> to come first, signs that tend to come in the middle, and signs that
> tend to come last — is not new, and it is not this program's
> discovery. Earlier researchers described that same broad pattern,
> including Wells and Fuls (2015), Sinha and colleagues (2010), Yadav
> (2009), and Fuls's work on classes of signs by position. What this
> program adds is something narrower: proposed meanings for signs in
> those positions, and checks, registered in advance, of how often the
> pattern appears in material that was not used to build the program's
> ideas. The script is not deciphered.

The research index page needs no change ("positional structure" there
is a topic label, not a novelty claim).

### Items 2–4 — no change needed unless the topics are ever added

Audit-consistent wording is on file in the fix addendum for that case:
the Farmer debate described as contested and unresolved; Nair 2026
described as an independent scorecard study with a cautious,
in-between conclusion (never a "replication"); Parpola (2010) cited as
the World Classical Tamil Conference lecture (Coimbatore, 25 June
2010, CICT Chennai), never as *World Archaeology* 42(2), 178–193,
which could not be verified.

## Limitation

On the Indus page, the "Study papers & key links" section was
extracted as a heading and intro only, with no bibliography entries in
the fetched text — so a bibliography entry carrying any of the four
items cannot be ruled out from this fetch alone. The check should be
repeated against that section's entries before the page's next edit.
