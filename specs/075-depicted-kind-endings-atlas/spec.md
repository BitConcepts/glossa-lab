## 1. The claim family and the record it stands on

### 1.1 The question, in plain words

Which kinds of depicted signs gather at the ends
of inscriptions? For each kind the published
identifications actually contain — human figures,
animals, objects and goods, and the residual
"other" — do that kind's signs end inscriptions
more often than signs of comparable frequency
from the other identified kinds?

The study mints its own claim family
(F075-C01…F075-C13, in this spec's register): the
depicted-kind positional-behaviour family. It is
not the Hunt sky-signs claim, it is not a claim
about Hunt's tripartite structure, and no outcome
under it is a verdict on either.

### 1.2 The falsifier, first

Per kind: a kind whose enrichment ratio (§5.2)
is at or below 1.0 is **not enriched at
endings** — its signs end inscriptions no more
often than signs of comparable frequency from
the other identified kinds. For the atlas as a
whole, the falsifiable shape is that *no*
identified kind is enriched at endings; four NOT
SUPPORTED words would state it, and that outcome
is named here in advance as a result this study
can honestly return. The falsifier is filed in
this spec's register at the freeze, before any
count exists (design R12).

### 1.3 The hypothesis

At least one identified depicted kind is
enriched at inscription endings under the
criteria of §5.2. The hypothesis is per-kind in
its testing: each kind stands or falls on its
own ratio, its own exact test, and its own
per-sign table, and no pooled "depicted signs"
claim is minted from the four (§5.2).

### 1.4 The named arm reported first: the celestial census

The celestial arm of this atlas is pre-registered
EMPTY (design R7). Reported first in every
outcome product, in this substance: the celestial
class as frozen in Asset A v2 contains **zero**
signs; there is no celestial rate; the empty
class is the Spec 071 line's closing finding
(retirement decision, 2026-10-25), restated here
because this atlas is where a reader would look
for it. The arm is not tested, sampled, or
counted as an arm of the battery; it establishes
nothing new about sky signs, and it does not
reopen, retest, or soften the retirement.

## 2. Ending, rates, and the comparison class

The machinery of this section is Spec 071's
§§4.2–4.3, applied kind by kind (design R10).
Where this section deviates from Spec 071's
text, the deviation is stated and ruled at
adjudication (DD-075-4, DD-075-5).

### 2.1 Ending

- A sign occurrence is in **text-final
  position** when it is the last sign of its
  inscription's recorded sequence in the
  primary population (Spec 071 §4.2, carried
  verbatim). "The ends of texts" is this
  position and no other: not the closing part
  of a tripartite formula, and not a
  suffix-like behaviour score — those are
  other designs' objects, named here so they
  cannot be conflated with this test.
- This spec introduces **no** new
  inscription-length floor, segmentation rule,
  sign ordering, or re-typing beyond the rules
  the primary population itself is built under
  (§3): sequences are the population's recorded
  sequences with the all-`000` placeholder rows
  excluded and `000` tokens dropped, exactly as
  the population was frozen for Specs 070/071.

### 2.2 Rates

- A sign's **final-position rate** is its
  final-position occurrences divided by its
  total occurrences in the primary population.
- A kind's **pooled final-position rate** is
  the kind's pooled final-position occurrences
  divided by its pooled total occurrences
  (occurrences pooled, not per-sign rates
  averaged — so a frequent sign carries its
  true weight, and §5.3's per-sign reporting
  exists precisely so that weight is visible).
- The **corpus-wide final-position rate** —
  pooled final-position occurrences of all
  signs in the primary population divided by
  all occurrences — is computed and published
  as descriptive context, and is the rate the
  per-sign condition of §5.2 uses. It is not
  the primary denominator for any kind: the
  primary comparison is the frequency-matched
  class of §2.3.

### 2.3 The comparison class, per kind

For each tested kind K, the **comparison class**
is the set of signs that are (a) identified
kinds other than K — their Asset A v2 kind is
one of the identified kinds (`other`, `human`,
`animal`, `object_goods`) and is not K's kind —
and (b) of **comparable frequency**: their total
occurrence count in the primary population lies
between the smallest and the largest total
occurrence count among K's member signs,
inclusive (Spec 071 §4.2's band rule, Spec
050's DD-050-2, applied with the band taken
from the kind under test).

**Stated deviation from Spec 071 §4.2 (design
R8, ruled DD-075-4):** `unidentified` signs are
**not** members of any comparison class under
this spec, inside the band or outside it. Spec
071's comparison class admitted unidentified
signs inside the band because its question was
one class against everything comparable; this
study's question is kind against kind, so the
comparison is the other *identified* kinds.
Unidentified signs are counted in the census
(§4.4) and in the corpus-wide rate (§2.2), and
no verdict word attaches to them.

Contested signs are not members of any class
or comparison class in the primary (Asset A v2
carries zero rows with `dispute_status =
contested`; §3). Borderline signs are members
of their kinds in the primary and are removed
only in sensitivity S1 (§5.4).

## 4. The classes and the floors

### 4.1 The classes

The classes are Asset A v2's depicted kinds
(§3). A sign's kind is the `depicted_kind` of
its one Asset A v2 row, keyed under §4.2. The
identified kinds are `other`, `human`,
`animal`, and `object_goods`. `unidentified`
is not a depicted kind (§4.4) and `sky_cycle`
is the empty celestial arm (§1.4).

### 4.2 Keying (design R2)

Every primary-population sign is keyed to
exactly one Asset A v2 row in Wells space by
the two-leg route of Spec 074 (DD-074-8),
adopted for this spec at DD-075-5: leg 1 — a
Shakti sign's ICIT code is its Wells number,
resolved through Registry v2's `parpola_1982`
rows' `wells_ids` to exactly one Asset A v2
`sign_key`; leg 2 — for a sign with no attested
Wells code under leg 1, Registry v2's
`icit_wells` rows are consulted, the row's
`sign_id` being the sign's own Wells code, to
the Asset A v2 row of that `sign_key`. Zero or
multiple resolutions across both legs is
unkeyable, and closes the execution at gate
0.4 as POPULATION NOT KEYED, the sign named —
with the single named exception of §4.3.
Keying is not classification: a sign that keys
to a row whose kind is `unidentified` is
counted, listed, and a member of no class.

### 4.3 Token 999 — the named exclusion (design R3)

Token 999, the one primary-population sign
with no published identity (Spec 073's NO
PUBLISHED IDENTITY schedule,
`specs/073-keying-surface-repair/registry-v2/no-published-identity.json`),
is a named exclusion from every class and
every comparison class under this spec. It is
not unkeyable in the sense of §4.2's closure —
it is excluded in advance, by name. Its
occurrences are counted and reported in the
step-0 census. Nothing in this spec states or
implies anything about what 999 is.

### 4.4 The unidentified signs (design R8)

Signs whose Asset A v2 kind is `unidentified`
(211 at asset level) enter no class and no
comparison class (§2.3). They are counted in
the step-0 census, and the corpus-wide
final-position rate over all signs is published
as descriptive context beside the census
(§2.2). No verdict word attaches to them, and
nothing in this spec states anything about
their behaviour as a group.

## 5. The per-kind test, the verdict words, and the falsifier

### 5.1 The per-kind test (design R10)

For each tested kind K: K's pooled
final-position rate (§2.2) is compared against
the pooled final-position rate of K's
comparison class (§2.3), by Fisher's exact test
(two-sided) on the pooled 2×2 table (K /
comparison × final / non-final occurrences),
at the 5% level (Spec 071 §4.3's machinery,
Spec 050's DD-050-4, carried). The executed
battery reproduces the frozen enumeration (the
step-0 census of §6) before any rate is
computed.

### 5.2 The verdict words (design R11)

Per kind, with R_K the kind's enrichment ratio
(its pooled final-position rate over its
comparison class's):

- **SUPPORTED** — R_K at least **1.5**, the
  exact test significant at the 5% level,
  **and** at least half the kind's total
  occurrences come from member signs whose
  individual final-position rates are above
  the corpus-wide final-position rate.
- **NOT SUPPORTED** — R_K at or below **1.0**.
  This is the falsifier's word (§1.2): the
  kind is not enriched at endings, at the rate
  signs of comparable frequency show.
- **INCONCLUSIVE** — anything else: a ratio
  between 1.0 and 1.5; or a ratio at or above
  1.5 that fails the exact test; or a ratio at
  or above 1.5 with the test passed but the
  half-of-occurrences condition failed.
- **TOO THIN** — the kind failed a floor at
  the step-0 census (§4.5), stated with its
  census and no rate.

Four kinds means four verdicts, each its own,
published together in one per-kind table with
every kind's per-sign table (§5.3). No pooled
"depicted signs" verdict exists under this
spec, and the family of four tests is stated
wherever the results are cited.

### 5.3 Per-sign reporting (part of the primary)

Alongside each kind's pooled result, every
member sign of the kind is reported in full:
its Asset A v2 verbatim label and citations,
its total occurrences, its final-position
occurrences, its individual final-position
rate, and whether that rate is above the
corpus-wide rate. One frequent sign cannot
carry a kind silently: the half-of-occurrences
condition (§5.2) is computed from this table,
and the table is published whichever way the
verdict lands (Spec 071 §4.4, carried).

### 5.4 Sensitivities (pre-registered, three; design R13)

All arms are run per kind and reported beside
the primary with the same prominence; none
replaces a primary verdict word. Agreement
across arms is not independence: the arms run
on compilations of substantially the same
excavated objects, and the outcome record says
so wherever the arms are reported.

- **S1 — Borderline removed.** Each kind's
  primary is rerun with every member whose
  Asset A v2 `dispute_status = borderline`
  removed. The removed signs are named in the
  census. (Asset A v2 carries 4 borderline
  rows; Spec 071 §4.5's S1, applied per kind.)
- **S2 — Contested included (Asset A's rule).**
  Each kind's primary is rerun with contested
  signs whose **CISI-governed** label (Spec
  045 §§2.5 and 5.3's inclusion rule) is an
  identification of that kind included in the
  kind under that label. Signs whose
  CISI-governed label is not of the kind do not
  enter. The included signs are named, with
  both of their published labels. (Asset A v2
  carries zero contested rows, so S2 is
  expected to equal the primary per kind and
  is reported as such where it does; Spec 071
  §4.5's S2, applied per kind.)
- **S3 — Corroborated sensitivity (P-F1 S4).**
  Each kind's primary is rerun over the
  documented + corroborated population (§3),
  with every quantity recomputed on that
  population; both populations' results are
  reported, and a verdict-relevant change
  between them is reported as grade-sensitive
  wherever the result is cited.

## 7. Controls and named confounders

- **Frequency.** A kind's raw final-position
  rate confounds kind with frequency: frequent
  signs end more inscriptions by count alone.
  The control is the design's: the comparison
  class is frequency-matched to the kind's own
  member band (§2.3), and the corpus-wide rate
  is context, never the denominator.
- **Single-sign dominance.** A kind carried by
  one frequent sign would pass a pooled test
  while saying nothing about the kind. The
  controls are the half-of-occurrences
  condition (§5.2) and the published per-sign
  tables (§5.3).
- **The family of tests.** Four kinds are
  tested under one design. No pooled claim is
  minted (§5.2), the four words are published
  together, and the family is stated wherever
  any one word is cited — a reader who meets
  one SUPPORTED word meets the family's other
  three words in the same record.
- **Classification disputes.** The kinds are
  the published identifications under Spec
  073's governing hierarchy, disputes carried
  in the asset's own fields. The controls are
  sensitivities S1 and S2 (§5.4), reported
  beside the primary, never folded into it.
- **Lineage.** Every population here is one
  ICIT-lineage corpus (§3). Agreement with any
  earlier ICIT-lineage result is consistency
  within a tradition, not independent
  corroboration; sensitivity S3 widens the
  grades within the same corpus and the same
  tradition, and is not an independence arm.

## 9. Epistemic limits (binding on all outputs)

1. **Position is not meaning.** Even a clean
   SUPPORTED shows a kind's signs cluster at

   cycles, trade, identity-marking, or any

   Parpola 1994). A verdict under this spec is
   a statement about that layer, on this
   corpus — depiction is the
   published-identification layer, not ground
   truth.
3. **The 49% caution travels with the asset.**
   Spec 037's census found object-*type*
   labels diverging between the CISI volumes
   and the programme's corpus in 49% of
   matched cases. Asset A's construction (held
   published sources only, blind entry, dispute
   by rule, freeze before join) is the design's
   answer to that caution — restated wherever
   Asset A v2 or a verdict under this spec is
   stated, never presented as a refutation of
   the census.
4. **One corpus, one lineage.** Every rate is
   a rate in the graded Shakti corpus, an
   ICIT-lineage corpus (P-F1 S3, stated in
   every headline block). Nothing here is a
   statement about any other compilation's
   numbering or counts, and agreement with the
   programme's earlier ICIT-lineage layer is
   consistency within one tradition, not
   independent corroboration.
5. **Sensitivities are not independence.**
   S1–S3 (§5.4) run on compilations of
   substantially the same excavated objects;
   their agreement with the primary is
   reported as agreement, never as
   corroboration from a second source.
6. **Spec 071's retirement stands untouched.**
   This spec's celestial arm (§1.4) republishes
   a settled finding in its proper place. It
   is not evidence about the retired question,
   it does not satisfy the retirement's
   reopening trigger, and no outcome under
   this spec amends, softens, or reopens the
   retirement or the claim's UNTESTED standing.
7. **The unidentified signs are a stated

8. Negative, inconclusive, too-thin, closed,
