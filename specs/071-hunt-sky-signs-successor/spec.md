## 1. The claim and the record it stands on

Hunt's three-part account of inscription
structure assigns the closing part to cycles
and calendars. Spec 050 was the third part's
design: the endings. Its question, its
falsifier, its celestial class (Asset A's
`sky_cycle` kind exactly, its §2.2 boundary),
its enrichment criteria, and its sensitivities
are carried into this successor unchanged in
substance (§4). What the re-scope changes is
stated in §1.1 of the readiness assessment's
terms and in this draft's §2–§3: the asset is
Asset A as built by Spec 069; the corpus is the
graded Shakti corpus, asserted at step 0 under
P-F1; and the ending classes are derived on
that corpus under the recovered battery,
applied new — not reproduced from the pinned
positional framework Spec 050's gate 0.5
required, a gate whose quantities belong to a
corpus the programme does not hold (Spec 045's
outcome record, gate 0.2, R5).

### 1.1 The question, in plain words

Hunt's closing part should be sky signs — sun,
star, moon — standing for cycles and calendars.
Which signs are sky signs is a visual judgement;
the published sign lists make it, and Asset A
records those judgements sign by sign. With
that class defined and frozen by Spec 069, and
with the ending positions measured on the
graded ICIT corpus, does the sky class actually
gather at the ends of inscriptions?

### 1.2 The falsifier, first

**Falsifier.** Celestial-class signs are not
enriched at text endings. Operationally, in
the design's own terms: the hypothesis fails
if the celestial class's final-position rate
is at or below the rate signs of comparable
frequency show — the enrichment ratio of §4.3
at or below 1.0.

Everything below is written so that this
falsifier can fire: the class is fixed by a
frozen external asset before any counting
(§4.1), the ending and enrichment measures are
defined before any counting (§4.2–§4.3), the
step-0 gates run before any final-position
quantity exists (§5), and every outcome —
including the falsifier's own word, NOT
SUPPORTED — is pre-written in §4.6 before any
result can be known.

### 1.3 The hypothesis

**Hypothesis.** Signs in the celestial class
are enriched in text-final position relative
to their overall frequency — stated precisely
in §4.3: the pooled final-position rate of the
celestial class exceeds the pooled
final-position rate of a frequency-matched
comparison class by a ratio of at least 1.5,
at the 5% level, with at least half the
class's occurrences coming from signs
individually above the corpus-wide final rate.

## 2. The positional battery — the Phase-43 rules, applied new to an ICIT corpus

The ending classes of this spec are derived
on the Shakti corpus under the rules of the
Phase-43 battery, recovered in full in the
battery record
(`~/workspace/research_notes/amendment-queue-2026-10-21/item-2-phase43-battery.md`,
§2, read first-hand at this draft) and stated
here as a battery **applied new to an ICIT
corpus** — a first measurement on this corpus,
not a reproduction of any Phase-43 quantity.
This is the same battery, stated in the same
words, as Spec 070 §2: the two successors'
classes may be computed in one battery run
family (banner), under one statement of the
rules.

- **Position rule.** For each sequence of
  length ≥ 2, each sign occurrence is counted
  once: position 0 → initial (I); last
  position → terminal (T); any position
  between → medial (M). A two-sign sequence
  contributes one I and one T and no M.
  Sequence order is the corpus's recorded
  order; no reversal is applied. Per sign:
  `total` = I + M + T; `i_rate` = I / total,
  `m_rate` = M / total, `t_rate` = T / total.
- **Count floor.** A sign enters classification
  only if `total` ≥ **5**.
- **Strength cascade.** First match wins, in
  this order: (1) `t_rate` ≥ **0.60** →
  TERMINAL_STRONG; (2) `i_rate` ≥ **0.55** →
  INITIAL_STRONG; (3) `m_rate` ≥ **0.65** →
  MEDIAL_STRONG; (4) `t_rate` ≥ 0.40 →
  TERMINAL_MODERATE; (5) `i_rate` ≥ 0.40 →
  INITIAL_MODERATE; (6) otherwise MIXED. The
  cascade order is part of the rule: a sign
  with `t_rate` ≥ 0.60 is TERMINAL_STRONG even
  if its `i_rate` also clears 0.55.
- **G5 attestation (binding wording).** The
  Phase-43 battery is **threshold-only**: it
  contains **no chance-baseline step** — no
  permutation, shuffle, or expected-rate
  baseline for its positional classes was ever
  part of it (battery record §2). This spec
  claims to restore none. Its comparison is
  the frequency-matched comparison class of
  §4.2 and the exact test of §4.3, which are
  this design's own test construction (carried
  from design D-13 and Spec 050), not a
  Phase-43 element, and no product may
  describe them as one.

Sequences are the primary population's
sequences (§3): inscriptions of the
documented-lineage rows, in ICIT sign codes,
counted in Wells space. The all-`000`
placeholder rows enter no sign analysis, per
the grade discipline of §3.

## 4. Definitions and the primary test

### 4.1 The celestial class and the ending population

- The **celestial class** under this spec is
  exactly the set of signs whose Asset A row
  carries `depicted_kind = sky_cycle`, keyed
  by the asset's primary key in Wells space
  (§3), one row per sign. Each member's
  standing rests on the fields Spec 045 §2.2
  fixes per row: the verbatim published label,
  its citations, the governing source, the
  `agreement` value, and the `dispute_status`.
  Only `sky_cycle` is a member of this class.
- **The boundary Spec 050 stated, carried.**
  This spec takes the narrower reading, as
  Spec 050 §2.2 stated it: the celestial notion
  is the sky — signs the published
  identifications describe as sun, star, moon,
  or celestial bodies, which is Asset A's
  `sky_cycle` kind. This spec does **not**
  widen the class to any jurisdiction-coded
  subset, to any sign whose verbatim label a
  reader might call celestial while its Asset
  A kind is `object_goods`, `human`, or
  `other`, or to any sign classified by a
  worker's own visual judgement. If Hunt's
  closing part also carries jurisdiction
  signs, that broader claim is **not tested
  here**; a verdict under this spec is a
  verdict about `sky_cycle` signs at endings,
  and its wording says so (§4.6, §8).
- **Contested rows (Spec 045's rule).**
  Contested Asset A rows are **excluded from
  the primary** and **reported** — counted,
  listed with both verbatim labels — and
  enter only in sensitivity S2 (§4.5), under
  the CISI-governed label rule stated there.
  `unidentified` signs are never celestial;
  they belong to the comparison population
  only as §4.2 states, and are counted and
  reported, never dropped silently.
- **The ending population.** The population
  frame of this test is **Shakti's own
  terminal-strong set**: the signs classed
  TERMINAL_STRONG by the §2 battery over the
  primary population (documented-lineage rows
  only), enumerated in full **in Wells space**
  and published at gate 0.4 — **before any
  test**. Its size is the battery's output on
  this corpus; it is not fixed in advance and
  no recorded count stands as its check.
  Every enumerated sign is keyed to **exactly
  one Asset A row in Wells space**, under the
  keying rule the freeze fixes (OPEN-071-3).
  A sign that cannot be so keyed is a **named
  step-0 finding** (gate 0.4): listed by its
  Wells identity, with the reason, and
  disposed of exactly as the freeze stated in
  advance (OPEN-071-4) — never quietly
  replaced, dropped, or hand-keyed. The
  per-sign table of §4.4 is published over
  this enumerated set and over the celestial
  class, so the class's behaviour at endings
  is visible sign by sign against the corpus's
  own ending-strong population.

### 4.2 Ending, and the comparison class

- A sign occurrence is in **text-final
  position** when it is the last sign of its
  inscription's recorded sequence in the
  primary population. "The ends of texts" is
  this position and no other: not the closing
  part of a tripartite formula, and not a
  suffix-like behaviour score — those are
  other designs' objects, named here so they
  cannot be conflated with this test. (The
  terminal-strong class of §4.1 is the
  battery's class over the same final
  positions, published as the population
  frame; the primary's rates are counted over
  occurrences, as below.)
- This spec introduces **no** new
  inscription-length floor, segmentation rule,
  sign ordering, or re-typing beyond the §2
  battery's own rules, which apply identically
  to the celestial and comparison classes.
- A sign's **final-position rate** is its
  final-position occurrences divided by its
  total occurrences in the primary population.
  A class's **pooled final-position rate** is
  the class's pooled final-position
  occurrences divided by its pooled total
  occurrences (occurrences pooled, not
  per-sign rates averaged — so a frequent sign
  carries its true weight, and §4.4's per-sign
  reporting exists precisely so that weight is
  visible).
- The **comparison class** is the set of signs
  that are (a) not celestial in the primary
  (their Asset A kind is not `sky_cycle`, and
  they are not `contested`), and (b) of
  **comparable frequency**: their total
  occurrence count in the primary population
  lies between the smallest and the largest
  total occurrence count among the primary
  celestial members, inclusive (Spec 050's
  DD-050-2, carried). `unidentified` signs
  inside the band are members of the
  comparison class and are counted and flagged
  as such in the census; they are never
  celestial.
- The **corpus-wide final-position rate** —
  pooled final-position occurrences of all
  signs in the primary population divided by
  all occurrences — is computed and published
  as descriptive context, and is the rate the
  per-sign condition of §4.3 uses. It is not
  the primary denominator: the primary
  comparison is the frequency-matched class,
  as Spec 050's adjudication records.

### 4.3 Enrichment, pinned (the criteria)

Let R_sky be the celestial class's pooled
final-position rate and R_cmp the comparison
class's pooled final-position rate. The
**enrichment ratio** is R_sky / R_cmp.
Significance is by **Fisher's exact test**
(two-sided, Spec 050's DD-050-4, carried) on
the pooled 2×2 table (celestial / comparison ×
final / non-final occurrences), at the **5%
level**.

- **SUPPORTED** — enrichment ratio at least
  **1.5**, the exact test at the 5% level,
  **and** at least half the celestial class's
  total occurrences come from member signs
  whose individual final-position rates are
  above the corpus-wide final-position rate.
- **NOT SUPPORTED** — enrichment ratio at or
  below **1.0**. This is the falsifier's word:
  the class is not enriched at endings, at the
  rate signs of comparable frequency show.
- **INCONCLUSIVE** — anything else: a ratio
  between 1.0 and 1.5; or a ratio at or above
  1.5 that fails the exact test; or a ratio at
  or above 1.5 with the test passed but the
  half-of-occurrences condition failed.
- **TOO THIN** — the §5 gate-0.4 closure: the
  celestial class, or the comparison class,
  totals fewer than **30** occurrences. TOO
  THIN is the recorded form of INCONCLUSIVE
  for a class too small to test (Spec 050's
  DD-050-3, carried); it is stated with its
  census and no rate.

The verdict word, whichever it is, is a word
about the **re-scoped** test: Hunt's claim
against a positional framework measured on
the graded ICIT corpus (the banner's honesty
fence). No wording under this spec presents
it as a verdict on the Phase-43 framework,
and no wording presents position as meaning.

### 4.4 Per-sign reporting (part of the primary)

Alongside the pooled result, every celestial
member sign is reported in full: its Asset A
verbatim label and citations, its total
occurrences, its final-position occurrences,
its individual final-position rate, whether
that rate is above the corpus-wide rate, and
its standing in the enumerated terminal-strong
population of §4.1. One frequent sign cannot
carry the class silently: the
half-of-occurrences condition (§4.3) is
computed from this table, and the table is
published whichever way the verdict lands.

### 4.5 Sensitivities (pre-registered, three)

All arms are run and reported beside the
primary with the same prominence; none
replaces the primary's verdict word.

- **S1 — Borderline removed.** The primary is
  rerun with every celestial member whose
  Asset A `dispute_status = borderline`
  removed. The removed signs are named in the
  census. (Spec 050's S1, carried.)
- **S2 — Contested included (Asset A's
  rule).** The primary is rerun with contested
  signs whose **CISI-governed** label (Spec
  045 §§2.5 and 5.3's inclusion rule) is a
  `sky_cycle` identification included in the
  celestial class under that label. Signs
  whose CISI-governed label is not `sky_cycle`
  do not enter. The included signs are named,
  with both of their published labels. (Spec
  050's S2, carried.)
- **S3 — Corroborated sensitivity (P-F1
  S4).** The primary is rerun over the
  documented + corroborated population (§3),
  with the battery classes re-derived on that
  population and every quantity recomputed;
  both populations' results are reported, and
  a verdict-relevant change between them is
  reported as grade-sensitive wherever the
  result is cited.

## 6. Controls and named confounders

- **Classification discretion — the whole
  ballgame, in the design's words.**
  Controlled by construction, and only by
  construction: the class comes from a frozen
  external asset built from published sources,
  blind, with a per-sign citation (§4.1); the
  sensitivities of §4.5 name the borderline
  and contested margins explicitly. No sign's
  membership is decided inside this spec.
- **Frequency confounding.** Sky signs may be
  rare or common as a class; final-position
  rates move with frequency. Controlled by the
  frequency-matched comparison class (§4.2)
  and by publishing the corpus-wide rate beside
  it, so the matching itself is inspectable.
- **One frequent sign carrying the class.**
  Controlled by the half-of-occurrences
  condition (§4.3) and the full per-sign table
  (§4.4).
- **Grade sensitivity by construction.** The
  corroborated stratum is systematically
  different material — more incomplete, more
  `000` placeholders, shorter, concentrated in
  different artefact types (provenance audit
  of 2026-10-22, §2) — so positional rates
  computed over it describe a different
  population. Controlled by the documented-only
  primary and sensitivity S3, never by pooling
  silently.
- **Excavation and corpus composition.** The
  corpus is one compilation's surviving record;
  site and object mix are described in the
  outcome record for the celestial and
  comparison occurrences, never adjusted away
  silently. This spec does not stratify by site
  or object type: design D-13 registers a
  class-level test, and adding strata would
  change its estimand.
- **Segmentation dependence.** Final position
  is defined on recorded sequences (§4.2); a
  differently segmented corpus could give a
  different answer, which is limit 5 of §8
  stated from the other side. The battery's
  rules bind this run to the stated
  conventions exactly.

## 8. Epistemic limits (binding on all outputs)

1. **Position is not meaning.** Even a clean
   SUPPORTED shows sky signs cluster at
   endings on this corpus. It does not
   establish scheduling, ritual calendars,
   cycle content, or any reading of any sign.
   The verdict wordings of §4.6 carry this
   sentence in substance, and no output may
   drop it.
2. **The class is the sky only.** Per §4.1,
   no jurisdiction-coded or otherwise
   celestial-adjacent sign outside Asset A's
   `sky_cycle` kind is tested. A verdict here
   neither supports nor falsifies Hunt's
   closing part in any broader form.
3. **Depiction is the published-identification
   layer, not ground truth.** Asset A records
   what the published lists say a sign depicts,
   with citations. A verdict under this spec is
   a statement about that layer, on this
   corpus.
4. **The 49% caution travels with the asset.**
   Spec 037's census found object-*type*
   labels diverging between the CISI volumes
   and the programme's corpus in 49% of matched
   cases. Asset A's construction (held
   published sources only, blind entry, dispute
   by rule, freeze before join) is the design's
   answer to that caution — restated wherever
   Asset A or a verdict under this spec is
   stated, never presented as a refutation of
   the census.
5. **One corpus, one lineage.** Every rate is
   a rate in the graded Shakti corpus, an
   ICIT-lineage corpus (P-F1 S3, stated in
   every headline block). Nothing here is a
   statement about any other compilation's
   numbering or counts, and agreement with the
   programme's earlier ICIT-lineage layer is
   consistency within one tradition, not
   independent corroboration.
6. **The honesty fence is a limit, not a
   preface.** The re-scoped programme tests
   Hunt's claims against a positional framework
   measured on the graded ICIT corpus; it does
   not test them against the Phase-43
   framework, and its outcome wordings must not
   cite 805/2,267 or 40/61/20 as their basis
   (banner; §3 of the reporting rules in §9).
7. **Contested signs are a stated exclusion.**
   The primary's class is the uncontested
   `sky_cycle` set. What contested signs would
   do to the result is answered only by
   sensitivity S2 (§4.5), reported beside the
   primary, never folded into it.
8. **Spec 050's record stands untouched.** Its
   frozen text and its not-run dependency
   closure are records of the Mahadevan-era
   design; this spec appends a successor
   beside them and edits neither. The frozen
   outcome of Spec 045 is likewise untouched.
9. Negative, inconclusive, too-thin, closed,
   invalid, and not-adjudicated outcomes
   publish with the same prominence as a
   supported one.
