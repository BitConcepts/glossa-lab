### 1.1 The question, in plain words

A claim about a sign cannot be carried in
the program's claim form until the sign it
names is identified — not by its number,
which different lists reuse for different
signs, but by its drawn form. The first run
found the argument and could not identify
the sign from what the program held. The
program now holds a preserved, hashed,
inspectable reference for the drawn sign,
derived in text from sources it lawfully
holds. Can the drawn sign be identified
from that reference, under a protocol that
checks the reference instead of trusting
it — and if it can, does candidate K-1 then
pass the whole recovery test and enter the
record as a claim, created and untested?

### 1.2 The hypothesis and its falsifier

number 821 labels can be identified under

preserved reference sustains the protocol's

identifies the sign (§5, step M) — so K-1
passes R-3, and with it the full recovery
test (§4).

## 5. The identification protocol (R-3)

The protocol is the amendment draft's §7
protocol as it applies to sign 821, frozen
here. Steps run in order: step 0 (§3),
step D, step C, step M.

### Step D — descriptor derivation, twice

Two independent derivations of the
drawn-form descriptor from the plate
(thesis p. 92 / PDF p. 104, produced at
gate 0.3), each performed **without sight
of the other and without sight of the
preserved record's drafted descriptor
wording**, each recording: the plate
locator, the feature list it read (stroke
count, outline, internal divisions, and the
doubling structure), and the neighbour set
it discriminated against (at minimum 818,
820, 822, 823, and 791, the discriminators
the preserved record names). Reconciliation
is by the recorded features; an unsettled
disagreement is recorded verbatim and
resolves **against** identification (the
DD-056-14 rule, carried). The reconciled
descriptor is then compared with the
preserved record's descriptor: **agreement
is required**; a material difference closes
the run as **IDENTIFICATION FAILED
(reference not sustained)**, naming the
difference. The drafted descriptor is
thereby *checked*, not inherited — the
Spec 055 rule that the draft's expected
reading has no standing.

### Step C — continuity check (821, by anchors)

The run states, from the record, what
anchors the plate number to the 2011/2015
numbering. For 821 the anchors are: the
formation passage (the 2015 book's own text
states sign 820 doubles to form sign 821);
the registry's 2015-form cross-match (W820
on P378's six-spoke-wheel description); and
the plate agreement (the thesis plate at
820 is a six-sector wheel in an oval, and
at 821 that wheel doubled — both agreeing
with the 2015-side records at the same
numbers). The check **passes on the
anchors' agreement**: the formation passage,
the registry anchor, and the two step-D
derivations must all agree on the doubled
six-spoke-wheel form at 820/821. If they do
not, the run closes as **IDENTIFICATION
FAILED (continuity not sustained)** — a
pre-registered closure, not a defect
discovered at scoring time. The plate
number by itself anchors nothing: a plate
number read without this step is a number
match, and is forbidden.

### Step M — the match (routes 1–3)

The drawn sign is identified by exactly one
of the following frozen routes:

- **Route 1 (registry, by drawn form).** A
  registry row whose drawn description
  matches the reconciled descriptor — never
  by number, and the frozen traps (§2) are
  asserted as traps in the run record. The
  composition descriptor is matched *as a
  drawn form* (a doubled six-spoke wheel),
  not via the W820 cell: if a registry row
  carries that drawn form, it is the match;
  if none does, route 1 fails and the run
  proceeds to route 2 — it does **not** fall
  back to P378, which is the base sign, not
  the target.
- **Route 2 (the list's own entry, through
  the preserved reference).** The preserved
  reference record itself, cited as the
  preserved drawn-form reference for the
  source list's entry — the route Spec 056's
  R-3 words already admitted, now
  satisfiable because the reference exists
  as a committed, hashed, inspectable
  record, sustained at step D and anchored
  at step C.
- **Route 3 (composition; 821 only; NEW).**
  The formation passage plus the base sign's
  registry drawn description, with the
  plate confirmation as its check. This
  route is frozen with its justification,
  stated here in the freeze exactly as the
  amendment draft's §7 states it: **a
  formation statement in the source's own
  appendix, anchored to a registry drawn
  description and confirmed on the author's
  own earlier plate, is a drawn-form
  identification and not a number match,
  because every step is a drawn form — the
  base sign's registry description, the
  doubling operation from the source's own
  appendix, the plate's drawn pair — and
  the number is used only to locate them.**

No route may be constructed at run time. A
run that finds none of its frozen routes
satisfiable closes as **IDENTIFICATION
FAILED**, naming the route(s) attempted —
the same closure the predecessor reached,
now with the reference of record in place.

**Traps asserted in every run record:**
`Yunmapped_0821`; sign 861 (Figure 6.12) as

## 9. Controls and named confounders

- **The drafter's descriptor as prior
  knowledge.** The preserved record carries
  a drafted descriptor; an executor who has
  read it cannot un-read it. Controlled by
  step D's form: two derivations performed
  without sight of the drafted wording, each
  recording the features it read, and the
  reconciled descriptor compared with the
  drafted one only after reconciliation —
  agreement is a finding, not a starting
  point, and a material difference closes
  the run against identification.
- **Number collision, including across list
  states.** Identical numbers name different
  signs in different systems (`Yunmapped_0821`
  is the standing trap) and in different
  states of the same author's list (587
  signs in 1998; 676 in 2006; 673 in 2011;
  694 in 2015). Controlled by R-3's routes,
  its number-matching ban, and step C: the
  plate number anchors nothing by itself,
  and continuity at 820/821 must be shown
  from the 2015-side anchors' agreement.
- **The base sign as the target.** P378 is
  sign 820's registry row. Controlled by
  step M route 1's no-fallback rule: the
  composition descriptor is matched as the
  doubled form, and P378 is never the
  target's match.
- **Reading confusion with sign 861.** The
  passage associates sign 861 with "city" as
  a different sign (Figure 6.12). Controlled
  by naming it as a trap in the preserved
  record and in every run record: an
  identification of 821 lends no support to
  any claim about 861, and no evidence about
  861 identifies 821.
- **Single-reader judgement.** Drawn-form
  matching is a judgement. Controlled
  structurally, as the predecessor's
  extraction was: two independent
  derivations, reconciliation by recorded
  features, and unsettled disagreement
  resolving *against* identification — the
  bias of the protocol runs toward the
  outcome that claims less. A match that
  cannot be defended from the recorded
  reference fails the identification step
  rather than being argued later (the Spec
  046 §7 inspectability control, carried).
- **Glyph loss in the held text.** The
  cleaned text drops sign glyphs and Figures
  2.6 and 6.13 are graphic-only in it (§2).
  That limit is the reason this spec exists
  and is not repaired by pretending
  otherwise: identification rests on the
  preserved reference and the pinned thesis
  plates, and every output states that
  basis.

## 10. Epistemic limits (binding on all outputs)

1. **Nothing about what sign 821 means.**
   This is identification and record repair,
   honestly labelled. Neither outcome
   decides the sign's reading, meaning,
   classification, or distribution:
   identifying which drawn sign K-1 names
   does not make K-1's suggested meaning
   true, plausible, or checked.
2. **Recovery is not verification.** A
   RECOVERED outcome establishes that a
   passage argues a proposition statable in
   the program's claim form, about a subject
   now identified. The proposition enters at
   created, untested (§7), and no output may
   borrow for it any credit from the act of
   recovering it.
3. **The argument conditions are of record,
   not re-decided.** R-1, R-2, R-4 and R-5
   stand as Spec 056's execution adjudicated
   them on the pinned passage. This spec
   neither strengthens nor weakens them; a
   reader who disputes them disputes Spec
   056's record, through that record's own
   channels.
4. **Closures are not outcomes about K-1's
   argument.** IDENTIFICATION FAILED and
   CLOSED AT STEP 0 state why the deciding
   act could not complete; neither may be
   reported as evidence for or against
   K-1's suggested meaning, and neither
   retires the fragment.
5. **The reference is a text record.** The
   descriptor is a derived factual record of
   what published drawings show, checkable
   by any party with lawful access to the
   cited published artefacts. It is not an
   image, not a reproduction, and not the
   list entry itself; route 2's use of it is
   as the preserved reference for the list's
   entry, stated openly wherever the outcome
   is stated.
6. Negative and closure outcomes publish
   with the same prominence as a recovery.

## 11. Risks and honest weaknesses

- **IDENTIFICATION FAILED is a live, real
  possibility the design prices in.** The
  preserved reference is a text descriptor;
  step D may not sustain it, step C may not
  hold, or no route may satisfy a hostile
  reader. A second closure, on the new
  record and naming its limb, is a result
  the record needs, not a failure of the
  run.
- **Continuity rests on agreement, not on a
  2015-side drawn form.** No record the
  program holds shows the 2015 list's drawn
  entry for 821 — that is the original gap.
  The continuity case for 821 is anchored
  from the 2015 side by two independent
  records (the formation passage and the
  registry cross-match) agreeing with the
  plate; it is stronger than a bare number
  claim and weaker than the entry itself,
  and §5 step C states it in exactly those
  terms rather than rounding it up.
- **The identification's centre is a
  judgement.** Drawn-form matching cannot
  be made mechanical. §9's controls make it
  inspectable and bias it toward claiming
  less; they do not eliminate it, and the
  outcome record must not pretend
  otherwise.
- **The thesis plates are an earlier state
  of the list.** The 2006 plates print 676
  signs; the fragments' list is the 2011
  form as given in 2015 (694 signs). Step C
  exists because of this risk, and its
  failure is a pre-registered closure, not
  an executor's improvisation.

## 14. What this spec is not

Not a re-extraction of the Dholavira
signboard passage, not a new test of K-1's
suggested meaning, not a corpus statistic,
not an identification of any other sign
(sign 297's successor is a separate spec,
outside this one), not a decipherment, and
not an authorisation to execute. It is the
successor identification act for one
candidate, designed so that its honest
outcomes — a claim recovered in proper
form, or a closure on the new record naming
the limb that failed — are both written in
advance, and the protocol, not the program,
chooses between them.

**This does not establish:** freezing the
reference does not establish that sign 821
is identified — identification is the
execution's step D/C/M work, which has not
run; Spec 056's IDENTIFICATION FAILED stands
as the record until a successor execution
replaces the outcome with its own.
