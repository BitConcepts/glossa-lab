# Note on the Shakti Intake Record — 2026-10-25 (programme red team, finding C-5)

This note is filed **beside** the intake record, not appended to
it: the intake record is a frozen input, pinned by the freeze
tests of Specs 052, 068, 070, 071, 074 and 075, and its bytes do
not change.

The grade **"documented-lineage"** in the intake record is a
**sequence-equality** measure: a row is graded documented when
its sign sequence occurs in the Yajnadevam/ICIT extraction under
the deposit's stated rule — not when that artefact's own reading
is documented. Verified by independent recomputation from the
deposit (programme red team, 2026-10-25): 3,212/5,704 rows match
(56.31%), with 0 mismatches against the row-grades file on all
5,704 rows. Two consequences every downstream use should carry:
(1) only 1,790 distinct sequences stand behind the 3,212
documented rows, and 1,571 of the 3,045 primary-population rows
(51.6%) share their sequence with at least one other corpus row,
so the grade certifies the sequence, possibly on a different
artefact; (2) the corroborated tier (2,087 rows) depends on the
programme's pre-existing ICIT-lineage layer, which is not in the
deposit, and is not reproducible from the deposit alone. Read
"documented-lineage" throughout the intake record as
"sequence-documented."
