Roles differ across workspaces

What is not in the list is not hinted at

Changed & why
Against wireframe 07: the collapsed “None open” program card becomes an open workspace card — current workspace “Indus Script · Admin”, the user’s other workspaces listed beneath it, each with its own per-workspace role badge. The specimen is built to prove two things at a glance: roles are per-workspace (Admin / Member / Viewer for one person, in one list), and absence leaks nothing — this edition also holds a specimen workspace the signed-in user has No access to, and it is entirely absent from the list: unnamed, uncounted, and not greyed out, in the list and in this screen’s body alike (it is named nowhere in this wireframe). The “All workspaces” manage entry appears because the signed-in user is an admin; it is not part of a member’s or viewer’s switcher.
