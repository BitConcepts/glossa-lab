# Spec 098 — Freeze Record v1 (2026-11-21)

**Frozen at this package's adoption, before any test
inscription was revealed to any scoring path and before any
scoring-path code ran. This record fixes the parameters the
spec's design (spec.md §3–§4) leaves to the freeze. A change
to any parameter below is a new freeze version, recorded as
such; there is no silent edit.**

## 3. Held-out set definition and seal

- Split rule: held out iff
  `int(SHA-256("098-split-v1:" + cluster_id)) mod 5 == 0`.
- Expected scale, measured at freeze time on the unclustered
  export as a proxy (cluster counts are struck at Stage 1):
  640 of 3,033 sequence-bearing records held out; 623
  population-sign tokens held out; 19 of 71 signs at or above
  the minimum count of §5.
- Seal: Stage 1 computes the held-out cluster list on
  tower-compute and commits a seal record carrying **only**
  the list's count and its SHA-256 (over the sorted cluster
  ids, one per line, UTF-8). The list itself is held in
  custody outside the repository (Spec 097 custody pattern)
  and is released to the scoring path exactly once, at
  scoring; the release is recorded in the run index. A seal
  mismatch at scoring voids the run: SEAL FAILURE, no
  per-sign outcomes.
- The reference implementation of this section is
  `tools/seal_and_score.py`; TC-098-01 exercises it.

## 5. Scoring and decision parameters

- Context model: value-conditioned sign-context model over
  neighbouring signs within the same inscription, built on
  development data only; order, smoothing constant, and the
  exact estimator are fixed in the Stage 1 execution freeze
  annex **before** the seal record is committed (they are
  execution details the design leaves open; the bars below
  are not open).
- Minimum held-out tokens per sign: **5**.
- Identification margin bar: penalized margin at least
  **10 nats** over the best rival.
- Permutation null: **B = 999**, seed **9801**; the margin
  must exceed the null's **99th percentile**.
- Flexibility penalty: **2 nats per free choice** (unresolved
  variant alternation, homophony allowance, gloss
  alternation), charged to candidates and rivals alike.
- Bootstrap for reported intervals: inscription-cluster
  bootstrap, B = 999, seed **9802**.
- Outcome vocabulary and precedence: spec.md §4
  (UNDECIDABLE AT THIS POWER; NOT IDENTIFIABLE;
  DISCRIMINATED ON PSEUDO-HELD-OUT for the in-corpus arm;
  IDENTIFIABLE ON HELD-OUT for the genuine-holdout subset
  only; SEAL FAILURE voids a run).
- Reporting: Standards Part II (effect size, interval,
  multiplicity family = the 71 signs, precision basis).

## 6. Venue and run discipline

Execution on **tower-compute only**, in a fresh clone under
`~/glossa-work/`; never the coordination VM, never the
mini-server. Every attempt indexed (Spec 089); every reported
verdict is the run's payload verdict record.

## 7. Named gaps at freeze time (why the study is not run in
the adoption round)

1. **Contamination / genuine holdout.** The candidates were
   derived on the program's full historical corpus; whether
   any sequence-bearing source in the corpus of record was
   demonstrably absent from every candidate-generation input
   is not established on the record at freeze time. Stage 1's
   provenance audit must establish the genuine-holdout subset
   or record its absence (spec.md §3.3, claim C098-6). Until
   then the in-corpus arm's ceiling is DISCRIMINATED ON
   PSEUDO-HELD-OUT, and striking the seal before the audit
   would put the cart before the record.
2. **M044 and M374 grapheme gap.** Neither sign is attested
   in the corpus export's canonical grapheme field at the
   pinned commit (69 of 71 signs attested; 2,955 population
   tokens). Stage 1 must resolve their sequences from the
   diplomatic text codes under the frozen crosswalk, or both
   default to UNDECIDABLE AT THIS POWER unscored.
3. **Power.** On the freeze-time proxy counts, 52 of 71 signs
   fall below the minimum held-out count on the in-corpus
   split. UNDECIDABLE AT THIS POWER is therefore the expected
   majority outcome of Arm I as frozen; the Stage 1 power
   grid states this per sign before unsealing, so the result
   cannot be re-narrated afterwards.

**Freeze verdict:** the design is FROZEN as above. The study
is NOT RUN in this round (decision D-098-2): gaps 1 and 2 are
Stage 1 work, and the seal is struck only after they are
resolved on the record.
