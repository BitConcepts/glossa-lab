## 2. The T101 mapping-table verdict (the reviewer's ruling basis)

The document's §6 maps its twelve definitions (D1–D12) to the WP4
text each applies. The finisher verified every citation against
`backend/glossa_lab/crosswalk_provenance.py` as it stands: the
docstring sections sit at the cited lines ("Exact primary-source
locator" line 39, "Comparison classifications" line 47,
"Eligibility" line 79, acceptance property (iii) line 97), the
`ORIGIN_*` constants at lines 129–133, `comparator_value()` line
161, `primary_source_locator()` line 173, `reading_value_origin()`
line 200, `classify_comparison()` line 249, and
`earns_independent_support()` exists at line 380. The five classes,
their precedence, the three-part locator test, the value-origin
vocabulary, and the eligibility rule (eligible ⟺
`independently_sourced`) are WP4's, restated without change.

One point required judgment, and it is named rather than buried:
§5's input construction — a valid coding (located, lineage-excluded,
unvoided) supplies the comparator value the recomputation
classifies, and a locator alone, with no coding, never lifts an
entry (worked consequence (c): located-but-uncoded stays at its WP4
class over the staged value). This is **not** a change to any WP4
class definition: the definitions, precedence, and tests are
untouched, and over the staged records alone the recomputation
reproduces WP4's classification of every entry exactly (the
document's stated anti-redefinition invariant). It is the design
act the grounding record's gap 3 names — the rule by which a
located, independently coded reading changes an entry's class —
exercised under C091-3's own text ("recomputed mechanically from
the locator record and the independent coding under WP4's
classification rules") and W1's ratified architecture, in which the
eligibility recomputation rests on the coding (C091-2's falsifier
presumes exactly that). A locator-only path to eligibility would
make the coding leg optional and re-admit the Phase-96 defect in a
new form: a stated source taken to carry a value nobody has read
there. **No WP4 class is redefined at any point in the document;
no correction to the draft was needed; no owner question arises.**
If the parent reviewer rules otherwise on this point, the ruling
and its consequence belong in the gating record, not in a silent
edit here.

## 4. T105's determination rule, and its one judgment

The rule (stated in the record): an anchor requires the
site-statistics arm iff its audit record's evidence vocabulary
(`evidence_tests`, or any support line's tests in
`evidence_independence`) names `distribution` or `functional_role`;
it requires the phonotactic arm iff that vocabulary names
`phonetic_value`, or its R5 rule text rests the defect on a check
of the reading's sound shape; an anchor engaging neither arm is
undeterminable and recorded as phonotactic-gated (plan.md Stage
1's conservative direction). The vocabulary half is mechanical
and is what the generator executes and the test recomputes. The
rule-text half was verified by reading the two rule texts the
record quotes verbatim: R5b (restored reading — the defect is a
missing derivation on the restored value's own record) and R5c
(Phase-48 battery cited as support — the defect is reliance on
the defective battery) rest on no check of any reading's sound
shape. Both texts are quoted in full in the record, so the
reading is checkable from the record itself. The outcome is
consistent with C091-10's own framing of candidate 2 (the
site-statistics lineage, `site_stats_v2`).
