Archive workspace — confirmation specimen

Archive “Comparative Scripts”?
Its 2 members lose access at their next request and it leaves their workspace switchers. Its claims, evidence and audit history are kept, read-only in the record — archiving is not deletion. An admin can restore it from this screen.

The Primary workspace cannot be archived while it is the edition’s primary workspace — its Archive action is absent, not merely disabled.

Annotation — wireframe 09 · not part of the UI
Purpose
Workspace administration: every workspace in the edition on one screen — membership size, the signed-in admin’s own role in it, status, and creation date — plus the create and archive acts in the same place.
Primary action
“+ Create workspace”, leading to the create form on this screen (name, description, initial members picker).
Changed & why
New surface for the owner’s workspace amendment: the earlier Program concept maps onto Workspace, so this is wireframe 04’s table language re-pointed at the tenancy unit itself. The seeded primary workspace, “Indus Script”, is the first row and is badged Primary; archiving gets a confirmation specimen because it revokes access for every member at once, and the Primary workspace carries no Archive action at all. The “Your role” column is deliberate: edition admin and workspace role are separate grants, and this screen shows the stored one rather than assuming it.
Spec claims
C083-16 (workspace creation is an admin act — this surface is admin-only and the form states there is no member-facing create path), C083-18 (workspace isolation — the lede states the tenancy rule where workspaces are administered, and an archived workspace leaves its members’ switchers).
