# Spec 041 — Coverage report (Phase-435 execution)

**The mapping covers the ICIT-lineage layer (horus84).**
Of the 78 distinct local chronological labels attested
on the 5,410 population inscriptions, 24 are placed in
a coarse period on a published determination verified
to the spec §3 bar (4 EARLY, 18 MATURE, 2 LATE) and 54
are UNASSIGNED with an enumerated reason; no cell is
CONTESTED (no cell drew two admissible determinations
assigning different coarse periods). At most 3,116 of
the 5,410 population rows (57.6%) carry any recorded
label at all, so no row-level use of this mapping can
place more than that share — and the mapping's own
coverage bounds every derivation further (§8 counts
below; this act adopts no derivation).

## Adjudications a reader should see plainly

1. **Nausharo Period IV is MAPPED MATURE, departing
   from the freeze's §5.4 illustration.** The example
   (NOT_IN_PUBLISHED_SCHEME) followed the Phase-139
   register's consultation, which reported the
   sequence ending at Period III. The excavator's own
   later published scheme, read in its own text
   (Jarrige et al. 2011, pp. 9, 30), contains Period
   IV as the third of the Indus civilization's main
   periods at the site, equated with Harappa 3C.
   Under the frozen standard the published evidence
   governs the cell; the departure and its evidence
   are recorded in the cell's note in
   `determinations.json`.
2. **Harappa 4/5 is a boundary span, not Late.** In
   the admissible scheme (Kenoyer & Meadow 2010;
   Meyer 2003), Period 4 is the Transitional unit
   across the Mature/Late boundary and Period 5 the
   Late phase, so no single coarse period is the
   publications' own assignment for the combined
   label. A joint Late assignment (Meadow & Kenoyer
   2001) exists in print but was reachable only
   through a website reprint without the article's
   pagination — reported, unverified, and preserved
   in the cell's determination set (DD-041-10).
3. **Harappa phases A, C, A-C, B/C were reclassified
   at dual verification** (below): the draft's
   scheme-inheritance from a IIIB-specific sentence
   did not survive a second reading.
4. **No CONTESTED cells arose.** Every candidate
   disagreement involved at least one strand that
   was inadmissible in venue or unverified at the
   bar (Harappa 4/5; Dholavira 3; Mohenjo-daro Late).
   The strands are preserved in `determinations.json`
   as the determination sets (DD-041-10), so a future
   freeze can re-open any of them on the record.

articles (*Asian Perspectives*; *International

locator aids or corroboration only; doubtful

and the Phase-139 register — locator aids only.
No source was reclassified after its evidence was
seen; the ASI-monograph application of §3.2(c) is
the one classification judgment the construction
required, and it is recorded here.

## What this report does not establish

The mapping translates recorded vocabularies on
published authority; it does not date any object,
repair any label's missingness, or judge Spec 040's
gate. Its counts sit under the 57.6% ceiling stated
above and must not be quoted without it. No
derivation is adopted or preferred; the row-level
choice belongs to a future Spec 040 freeze.
