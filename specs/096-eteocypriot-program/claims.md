# Spec 096 — claims

Four claims, C096-1..C096-4, each with its falsifier and its
does-not-establish line. The claims re-express, in this
program's own claim form, the draft claims EC-D1..EC-D4 of the
Eteocypriot preregistration draft of 2026-11-17 (Spec 095
Stage 5 second-target scoping; the founding record, cited as
provenance in spec.md). Nothing here re-opens any Spec 095
claim or any Indus-program claim.

## The claims

**C096-1 — The alignment procedure is validated by known
answers.** Under the registered alignment assumptions (A1
strict segment-parallel; A2 name-anchored free alignment), the
frozen alignment procedure recovers the bilingual name
correspondences of the development set (preregistration P1:
both ICS 196 correspondences and at least one Androkles
correspondence, at segment level, with no name information
supplied to the procedure beyond the Greek texts themselves).

*Falsifier:* P1 fails under both A1 and A2 — the procedure is
not validated, and E2 does not run.

*Does not establish:* that any Eteocypriot word is understood;
E1 tests the procedure against Greek-side ground truth only.

**C096-2 — At least one development-set candidate predicts
held-out material.** At least one registered morpheme or
recurring-ending candidate, developed only on the development
set, meets P2's success side (its registered held-out
occurrence floor at its registered positions).

*Falsifier:* every registered candidate is NOT SUPPORTED or
TOO THIN at scoring.

*Does not establish:* that a surviving candidate's proposed
meaning is correct — survival is a prediction result; meaning
requires E3 and adjudication.

**C096-3 — Surviving candidates are identifiable or are
reported as not.** Every E2 survivor's standing is set by its
E3 rival-construction record (P3): a survivor with an equally
consistent incompatible rival is recorded NOT IDENTIFIABLE,
whatever its E2 score, and no survivor is scored SUPPORTED
without a completed rival-construction record.

*Falsifier:* a survivor is scored SUPPORTED with no
rival-construction record, or with an equally consistent rival
unrecorded as NOT IDENTIFIABLE.

*Does not establish:* that a NOT IDENTIFIABLE candidate is
false (Standards Part III, rule 5).

**C096-4 — The study reports its own thinness honestly.**
Every outcome of the pilot is stated with its precision basis
(Standards Part II), and any leg below its registered floor is
recorded TOO THIN, including closures at freeze under floors
that cannot be instantiated against the frozen census.

*Falsifier:* a result is reported without its precision basis,
or a below-floor leg is scored as a result.

*Does not establish:* that any outcome so reported is
substantively correct; the claim governs the study's
reporting, not its results.
