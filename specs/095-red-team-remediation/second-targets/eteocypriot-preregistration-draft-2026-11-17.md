## 1. Research question

Can the program's decipherment machinery — corpus identity and
fail-closed loading, claim form with pre-fixed falsifiers,
held-out prediction, rival-construction identifiability, and
named-human adjudication — produce, for Eteocypriot (a known
syllabary writing an unknown language, with bilingual evidence),
at least one grammatical or lexical result that survives its
own falsifier on held-out inscriptions; and where it cannot,
does it correctly return NOT IDENTIFIABLE or TOO THIN instead
of a confident answer?

## 2. Foreknowledge statement

Known before any analysis under this draft, and relied on as
inputs, not results:

- The Cypriot syllabary's sign values (settled since the 19th
  century; proposal §4.2). The script is read; the language is
  not.
- The Greek sides of ICS 196 and of the Androkles dedications,
  including the name correspondences (Ariston / Aristonax;
  Orestheus / Andragoras) — the study's only ground truth.
- The editions of record and the corpus facts stated in the
  proposal (§3), including that ICS 196's stone is lost and its
  Eteocypriot version is not a literal translation of its Greek
  version.
- The program's standards: Standards of Record Parts I–III;
  the corpus identity standard (C095-3); the identifiability
  outcome vocabulary (NOT IDENTIFIABLE, per the 2026-11-16
  amendment).

Not computed at drafting, and not to be computed before the
execution freeze: any statistic over any Eteocypriot corpus;
any segmentation, alignment or morpheme candidate. This draft
was written from the published scholarly record alone.

## 3. Study design

### 3.1 Step 0 — corpus acquisition and freeze (fail-closed)

1. Corpus-availability survey (the proposal §3.3 unverified
   items resolved or carried as named gaps): editions, IG XV
   1,1 coverage and terms, any machine-readable source and its
   license.
2. Intake of the delimitation of record (proposal §3.4:
   Amathus primary stratum; Golgoi separate; no default
   inclusion of merely-uninterpreted texts) under an identity
   record per C095-3: version, content hash, source, licensing
   terms, counts under a named counting basis, normalization
   rules (including the transliteration scheme of record and
   the treatment of damaged/supplied signs), orientation basis
   (right-to-left, carried as data per C095-10).
3. Freeze: the corpus, the sign list of record, and this
   design are pinned by hash in the execution freeze record.
   **Closure rule: any identity mismatch, any licensing bar, or
   any delimitation dispute unresolved at freeze closes the
   study at step 0 with that as its outcome record** (the
   program's step-0 closure pattern).

### 3.2 The split

- **Development set:** ICS 196; the Androkles pair; the Greek
  syllabic control corpus (same signary, known language).
- **Held-out set:** every other primary-stratum inscription,
  including ICS 194/195 under Perna 2019's readings. Sealed at
  freeze: no held-out text is loaded into the analysis
  environment until the §4 predictions are registered.
- Split by inscription only; ICS 194/195 (the one long text) is
  its own stratum. Segmentation alternatives for every text are
  generated by the frozen procedure and carried as data (model
  G4), never chosen by inspection of outcomes.

### 3.3 Experiments

- **E1 — known-answer validation (development set).** The
  frozen alignment procedure is run on ICS 196 and the
  Androkles pair under the two registered alignment assumptions
  (§5). Success is the recovery of the known name
  correspondences at segment level. E1 validates the procedure;
  it produces no Eteocypriot finding.
- **E2 — held-out prediction.** Morpheme and recurring-ending
  candidates developed *only* on the development set are
  registered as predictions (form, predicted held-out
  occurrences and positions) before the seal is broken, then
  scored mechanically against the held-out set.
- **E3 — identifiability.** For every candidate surviving E2, a
  rival construction is attempted under Standards Part III
  (WP8 control families: meaning permutations, frequency- and
  position-matched wrong mappings, plausible wrong morphology,
  wrong-language candidates, structured non-language corpora),
  under matched tuning budgets. The outcome — SUPPORTED at a
  stated strength, NOT SUPPORTED, NOT IDENTIFIABLE, or TOO
  THIN — is recorded per candidate.

### 3.4 Controls

- The Greek syllabic control corpus (§3.2): the same pipeline
  run where the language is known; a method that "finds"
  Eteocypriot-like structure in Greek control material at the
  same rate has found its own artifact, and E2's scoring is
  read against the control rate.
- Permutation and non-language controls per E3's families.
- The contamination class (C095-7) runs over every intake:
  no derived alignment may re-enter as independent evidence;
  the Idalion bilingual is sign-value evidence only (proposal
  §4.1).

## 4. Registered predictions

Fixed by this draft in form; the execution freeze instantiates
the numeric floors against the frozen corpus census and records
them before any analysis. (A floor that cannot be instantiated
because the census is smaller than the floor's basis closes the
affected experiment as TOO THIN at freeze — stated now so the
closure cannot be mistaken for a result.)

- **P1 (E1).** Under at least one registered alignment
  assumption, the procedure recovers both ICS 196 name
  correspondences and at least one Androkles name
  correspondence, at segment level, with no name information
  supplied to the procedure beyond the Greek texts themselves.
- **P2 (E2).** Each registered morpheme/ending candidate either
  meets its registered held-out occurrence floor at its
  registered positions, or is recorded NOT SUPPORTED (absent)
  or TOO THIN (below floor) — no third, softer outcome exists.
- **P3 (E3).** Every E2 survivor carries a completed
  rival-construction record; a survivor with an equally
  consistent incompatible rival is recorded NOT IDENTIFIABLE,
  whatever its E2 score.

## 5. Alignment assumptions (registered rivals)

- **A1 — strict segment-parallel:** the bilingual versions are
  aligned segment-by-segment in order. (Expected to strain:
  ICS 196's Eteocypriot version is roughly twice its Greek
  version's length.)
- **A2 — name-anchored free alignment:** alignment is anchored
  on the known name correspondences and is otherwise free,
  with unmatched Eteocypriot material carried as unmatched,
  not forced.

Both assumptions are run; results are reported per assumption
(Standards Part II), and a candidate that survives under only
one assumption is reported as assumption-bound.

## 6. Populations and strata

- **Primary stratum (Amathus):** the Eteocypriot-defining
  inscriptions of Amathus, per the delimitation of record —
  expected 20–30 inscriptions program-wide, most from Amathus
  (proposal §3.1); the frozen census at step 0 replaces this
  expectation with the count of record.
- **Golgoi stratum:** held out of the primary analysis;
  includable only as sensitivity S2.
- **Control population:** Greek syllabic inscriptions under
  the same signary, sampled by the frozen procedure.

## 7. Draft claims (inside this draft only; never intaken)

- **EC-D1 — the alignment procedure is validated by known
  answers.** Under the registered assumptions, the frozen
  procedure recovers the bilingual name correspondences (P1).
  *Falsifier:* P1 fails under both A1 and A2 — the procedure is
  not validated, and E2 does not run.
  *Does not establish:* that any Eteocypriot word is understood;
  E1 tests the procedure against Greek-side ground truth only.
- **EC-D2 — at least one development-set candidate predicts
  held-out material.** At least one registered candidate meets
  P2's success side. *Falsifier:* every registered candidate is
  NOT SUPPORTED or TOO THIN at scoring. *Does not establish:*
  that a surviving candidate's proposed meaning is correct —
  survival is a prediction result; meaning requires E3 and
  adjudication.
- **EC-D3 — surviving candidates are identifiable or are
  reported as not.** Every E2 survivor's standing is set by its
  E3 record (P3). *Falsifier:* a survivor is scored SUPPORTED
  with no rival-construction record, or with an equally
  consistent rival unrecorded as NOT IDENTIFIABLE.
  *Does not establish:* that a NOT IDENTIFIABLE candidate is
  false (Standards Part III, rule 5).
- **EC-D4 — the study reports its own thinness honestly.**
  Every outcome is stated with its precision basis (Standards
  Part II), and any leg below its registered floor is recorded
  TOO THIN. *Falsifier:* a result is reported without its
  precision basis, or a below-floor leg is scored as a result.

## 8. Criteria and outcome classes

Outcome classes: SUPPORTED (at a stated strength, E3-clean),
NOT SUPPORTED, NOT IDENTIFIABLE, TOO THIN, and step-0 CLOSED
(§3.1). Statistical reporting per Standards Part II in full —
effect size, named interval construction, multiplicity family
(the registered candidate set is the family), precision basis —
and equivalence only per Part I. No outcome is a translation
claim: the strongest available statement is a single adjudicated
grammatical constraint or lexical interpretation (proposal §1).

## 9. Sensitivities

- **S1 — readings:** ICS 194/195 scored under Perna 2019
  (primary) and under Masson's readings (sensitivity), results
  reported for both.
- **S2 — Golgoi:** E2/E3 re-scored with the Golgoi stratum
  included as its own stratum; never merged into Amathus.
- **S3 — alignment:** all results reported per §5 assumption.

## 10. Adjudication and filing (execution-stage, not performed)

Standings are set only through the route the proposal names
(§6): ledger intake in claim form, hash-pinned evidence in the
registered classes, named-human approval, the expert
adjudication package as owner-approved external evidence. If
the owner commissions execution, this draft is filed as the
study's preregistration under the program's standing route
(the programme's OSF preregistration project) at the execution
freeze — **filing is an execution act and an external act; it
is not performed by this draft, and approval of any filing is
the owner's act.**

## 11. Pins

None exist yet, by design: there is no corpus to pin. The
execution freeze creates the pins (corpus identity hash, sign
list of record, this document's hash as registered) and records
them in the freeze record. A pin asserted before the corpus
exists would be a fabrication, and this draft asserts none.
