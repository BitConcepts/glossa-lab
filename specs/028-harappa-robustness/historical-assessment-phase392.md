## The changed reading

Phase-392's INCONCLUSIVE verdict left one specific
ambiguity: its jackknife clause failed on the Harappa
drop, which is consistent with two different worlds —
(a) the site-linked structure is broadly distributed and
Harappa merely carries the most weight, or (b) the
margin-clearing structure is Harappa's own.

Phase-393 was designed to separate those worlds, and it
does, descriptively, under two estimands:

- **World (a) is not supported.** As a primary
  population, the five non-Harappa sites produce a point
  estimate BELOW the frozen margin (0.00878), with every
  leave-one-out median also below it. Whatever structure
  exists among the non-Harappa sites at this design's
  resolution does not reach the practical margin the
  program froze.
- **The "big-corpus artifact" explanation is not
  supported either.** Equal site weighting — which strips
  the large corpora of their count advantage — raises the
  full-population estimate (0.02237 vs 0.01677). The
  counts were masking, not manufacturing, the effect.
- **What remains is Harappa-dependence.** Under both
  weightings, removing Harappa collapses the estimate
  below the margin (0.00928 count-weighted; 0.00661
  equal-weight), and no other single removal does.
  Arm C's decomposition localises the dependence:
  Harappa's distance from the other sites is carried by
  its TAB (tablet) repertoire (within-TAB Bray–Curtis
  0.4953 vs within-SEAL 0.1114), with a single column
  (M328) contributing ≈21.5% of the total distance.
