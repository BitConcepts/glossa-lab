from the Spec 086 Stage 5 record because the T602

## What this act does not do

**This record commissions nothing.** Ratification
settles the program's gap picture — the entries, their
ranking, and their dispositions are now the ratified
ones. First-wave execution is commissioned from the
ratified register separately, by a later owner act,
entry by entry or as a wave; no candidate in the
register is authorized for execution by this act. No
claim's status, stage, or score was changed by the
register, the scans behind it, or this ratification:
the ledger's hash before and after this closeout PR is
identical (see the T701 closeout record).

*This record does not establish:* that any register
entry's candidate will succeed, or that the register is
complete in nature — only that the owner's ratification
act was performed on register v1 as consolidated, with
its completeness verdict (T602) on the record.
