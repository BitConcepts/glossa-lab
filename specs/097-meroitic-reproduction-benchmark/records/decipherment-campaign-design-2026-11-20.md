# Meroitic Decipherment Campaign — Design and Preregistration (Spec 097, Stage 4)

**Date: 2026-11-20. Status: PREREGISTERED before any campaign
execution. Owner direction (2026-11-20, in chat): the program's
aim is decipherment, not only reproduction of the 2025 study —
"we want to work for decipherment, not just rerunning the study
from before. we are interested in doing whatever it takes to
get decipherment." This design is the program's answer, inside
its evidentiary discipline: readings are earned through the
registered tests below or recorded as NOT IDENTIFIABLE, never
asserted past the evidence. The reproduction benchmark
(Stages 1–3) stands as the campaign's foundation: it validated
the pinned corpus, fixed the published baselines, and its
verdicts are unchanged by anything in this stage.**

## 1. What "decipherment" means here, stated plainly

The Meroitic **script** was deciphered between 1907 and 1911
by F. Ll. Griffith: the 23 signs (plus the word divider) have
known sound values, so any inscription can be transliterated
and read aloud. What is **not** deciphered is the **language**:
there is no bilingual text of any size, the language left no
descendants, and present scholarship can translate no more
than about a hundred words with some certainty (Rilly 2016,
UCLA Encyclopedia of Egyptology), of which only a few dozen
lexemes carry confirmed meanings (Rilly & de Voogt 2012).
"Decipherment" for this campaign therefore means, in order of
increasing strength:

1. **Corpus decipherment-readiness** — the fullest lawfully
   obtainable machine-readable corpus, with every gap named.
2. **Anchored partial reading** — individual words, titles,
   formulae and constructions whose readings are pinned by
   independent evidence and pass the identifiability protocol
   (§5), each scored exactly as the evidence reads.
3. **Affiliation adjudication** — the language-affiliation
   question decided, or shown undecidable, by registered
   tests against named rivals (§4), because affiliation is
   what licenses (or forbids) comparative reading at scale.
4. **Connected-text translation** — the honest ceiling. On
   present evidence this campaign does **not** expect to
   deliver connected translation of non-formulaic texts, and
   any stage that finds its readings NOT IDENTIFIABLE will
   record exactly that. A campaign that ends with a complete
   anchor inventory, a completed corpus, an adjudicated
   affiliation, and a register of not-identifiable readings
   has done real decipherment work; a campaign that asserts
   readings the evidence cannot pin has done none.

## 2. Corpus completion (claim C097-7)

**Held now** (step-0 pin, custody): the Otten corpus —
mero-corpus.txt (18,090 lines / 782,761 words on the \w+
basis), the Millet/Rilly/Lobban/Kalabsha example files, the
seven bilingual dictionaries, the authors' published
embeddings. The benchmark's Leg A established that this is
not the full published record.

**The full record, as the literature states it:** the
Répertoire d'Épigraphie Méroïtique (REM, Leclant et al. 2000,
with appendices) publishes some 1,300 texts; roughly 900
further known texts (Qasr Ibrim, Musawwarat es-Sufra) remain
unpublished in the REM; total known inscriptions exceed
2,000. About half the published corpus is funerary; the
longest documents are a few dozen royal inscriptions.

**Acquisition plan, in order:** (i) consolidate everything
already lawfully held into one canonical corpus build with a
per-text provenance register (source, REM number where the
source gives one, genre, license state); (ii) acquire every
further openly licensed machine-readable Meroitic source
(public repositories and deposits whose licenses permit
scholarly use), each pinned and license-recorded exactly as
step 0 was; (iii) name every gap that cannot lawfully be
closed — expected gaps, registered in advance: the REM's own
digital edition (in progress, not openly licensed for bulk
use), the ~900 unpublished REM texts, Trismegistos' Meroitic
records (membership-gated), and the 2025 paper's unpublished
intermediates (already named in Leg A). No purchase and no
outreach is authorized by this design; a gap that only a
purchase or a license negotiation could close is recorded as
an owner decision, not worked around.

**Stage gate:** the corpus stage closes with a corpus
completion record stating, per source class: held / acquired
/ gap-named, with counts reconciled against the REM totals
above. The anchor freeze (§3) is taken against the corpus as
completed at that point; later acquisitions reopen the
inventory only by a new freeze version.

## 3. The anchor inventory (claim C097-8)

An anchor is a form whose reading is pinned by evidence
independent of the corpus statistics the campaign will test.
Every anchor carries an evidence class, and the class bounds
what the anchor may be used for:

- **Class A — name correspondences.** Proper names attested
  both in Meroitic and in Egyptian (hieroglyphic/Demotic) or
  Greek rendering: the royal and private names through which
  Griffith fixed the sign values, and the endonym *qes/qos*
  "Kush" (Egyptian *kꜣš*) and *bedewe* "Meroe" (Egyptian
  *bꜣ-rꜣ-wꜣ*). Class A anchors pin phonology and onomastics;
  they do not pin meanings of common words.
- **Class B — formula structure.** The funerary formula,
  known in outline since Griffith (1911) and present in about
  half the published corpus: invocation to Isis (*wosi*) and
  Osiris (*asiri*); "this is [deceased]"; [mother's name] +
  *teɖe*-form "born of"; [father's name] + *terike*-form
  "begotten of"; offering clauses (water, food); closing
  evaluation. The slot structure is the anchor; the exact
  morpheme segmentation of the filiation forms remains
  debated in the literature and is carried as debated, not
  frozen as fact.
- **Class C — numerals.** The numeral system is fairly well
  known (units, tens, hundreds, thousands attested; the signs
  for 60, 90, 900 and several thousands unattested), and some
  70 ostraca carry short administrative texts with numbers —
  the corpus's most nearly bilingual genre, because the
  arithmetic constrains the reading.
- **Class D — Egyptian loanwords and titles.** The stratum of
  titles and religious vocabulary borrowed from Egyptian
  (Rilly 2016: the translatable hundred is mostly titles,
  toponyms, and gods' names, many borrowed). Each Class D
  entry names its proposed Egyptian source; a loanword anchor
  pins a form's meaning only as far as the borrowing is
  demonstrated, not by resemblance alone.
- **Class E — contextually pinned common words.** The small
  set of common words the philological tradition translates
  with some certainty (e.g. *qor* "ruler", *kdi* "woman,
  lady", *ste* "mother", *mk* "god", *ato* "water"), each
  carrying its attestation contexts. Class E is the weakest
  class and is used for scoring controls, never as the sole
  support for a new reading.

**Freeze discipline:** the inventory is frozen as a versioned
record (v1 at Stage 4.1) listing every anchor's form, proposed
reading, evidence class, source, and the use it licenses. A
candidate reading that depends on an anchor not in the freeze
is out of scope until a new freeze version admits it.

## 4. The affiliation question, as testable rivals (claim C097-9)

Affiliation is carried as rivals with falsifiers, not as a
premise — including the leading hypothesis, which parts of
the literature present as settled. This program takes no
position by authority; it registers tests.

- **Rival R1 — Northern East Sudanic (Rilly).** Meroitic
  belongs to the Northern East Sudanic branch of Nilo-Saharan,
  with Nubian, Nara, Tama and Nyimang (Rilly 2004–2016).
  *Registered tests:* systematic sound correspondences on the
  anchor-frozen vocabulary against reconstructed NES forms;
  shared morphological formatives (the suffixing profile, the
  filiation and determiner formatives) under regular
  correspondence, scored against chance-resemblance baselines
  by permutation. *Falsifier:* the correspondence set on the
  frozen vocabulary does not beat the permutation baseline at
  the registered threshold, or the claimed shared formatives
  fail regularity on the full anchor set.
- **Rival R2 — Afroasiatic (Rowan; Lipiński).** Meroitic's
  phonotactics (Rowan 2006/2011: the near-absence of
  same-articulator CVC sequences, an Afroasiatic-style
  restriction) and vocabulary (Lipiński 2011) point to
  Afroasiatic affiliation. *Registered tests:* the
  phonotactic restriction measured on the completed corpus
  against both families' profiles; vocabulary comparison
  under the same permutation discipline as R1. *Falsifier:*
  the restriction does not hold on the completed corpus at
  the registered strength, or the vocabulary set does not beat
  baseline.
- **Rival R3 — Isolate / null.** No affiliation is
  demonstrable on present evidence (the standing classification
  in reference works that list Meroitic as unclassified).
  R3 is the null every other rival must beat: it is *supported*
  exactly when R1 and R2 both fail their registered tests, and
  it is falsified by either rival passing.
- **Not carried, with cause:** the Saharan proposal (Winters)
  and the Tocharian proposal are named in the literature but
  are not carried as registered rivals: neither has been
  taken up by the specialist community, and the design's
  permutation discipline is the route by which any future
  proposal, including these, earns registration — by beating
  the baseline on the frozen vocabulary in a registered
  amendment, not by assertion.

An affiliation verdict licenses comparative candidate
generation (§5) from that family only. If R3 stands, candidate
generation proceeds on internal evidence alone (formula,
numerals, distribution), and the expected yield is stated in
advance to be low.

## 5. Candidate readings: generation, identifiability, blind tests (claim C097-10)

**Generation.** Candidate readings for unanchored forms are
generated only from licensed sources: (i) comparative —
cognate proposals from the adjudicated affiliation family,
under the correspondence rules the affiliation stage froze;
(ii) internal — distributional and formula-slot analysis on
the completed corpus (a form's slot in the funerary formula,
its numeral contexts, its co-occurrence profile); (iii)
loanword — Egyptian source proposals under Class D discipline.
Every candidate is filed with its generation route and its
full evidence before scoring.

**Identifiability protocol (applied BEFORE any SUPPORTED
score).** A candidate reading is identifiable only if the
evidence pins it against its rivals: the record must show
(1) the candidate's predicted distribution across the corpus
differs from every named rival reading's prediction on at
least one observable dimension (slot, collocation, numeral
context, correspondence regularity); (2) the discriminating
observations actually occur in the corpus at the registered
minimum count; and (3) no equally simple rival survives the
same observations. A candidate failing any leg is recorded
**NOT IDENTIFIABLE** — the expected outcome for most
candidates on a corpus of this size and genre skew, stated in
advance so the outcome is a finding, not a failure of nerve.

**Scoring.** Surviving candidates are scored under the
program's standards (SUPPORTED / PARTIALLY SUPPORTED /
UNSUPPORTED / NOT IDENTIFIABLE), with the score's evidence
cited per observation. SUPPORTED additionally requires the
blind test below.

**Blind held-out test.** Before scoring opens, the corpus is
split: a development set (the formulaic funerary majority and
the texts used for generation) and a **sealed held-out set**
(complete inscriptions, chosen by genre-stratified rule in the
freeze, whose transliterations are not used in generation).
Readings are frozen in writing; the sealed set is then opened
once, and each frozen reading's predictions (slot occupancy,
collocates, numeral agreement) are scored against it. A
reading that fails on the sealed set is downgraded on the
record, whatever its development-set score. The seal is
procedural (hash-pinned split in the freeze record), because
the corpus is public and the discipline is the program's own.

## 6. Expert verification — design only (claim C097-11)

No outreach is made under this design. Prepared for the
owner's approval, in a later stage: a verification panel drawn
from the Meroitic specialist community (the REM editorial
circle and Sudanese-epigraphy specialists; comparative
linguists for the affiliation verdicts), offered (a) the
frozen anchor inventory for confirmation or challenge before
candidate scoring, and (b) the scored candidate register for
independent adjudication after the blind tests. The design
specifies: what each reviewer sees (frozen records, not
drafts), the response instrument (per-item confirm / challenge
with cause / no position), how challenges enter the record
(as evidence against the item, answered in writing), and the
standing rule that expert silence is recorded as silence,
never as assent. Names, letters, and any contact await the
owner's separate approval, as with Spec 091's engagement
discipline.
