## Why (a) serves the programme's success — the reasoning on record

Spec 072's spec §7 limit 1 (the Roif gate) permits the study to be named
in programme reporting only as "executed — held under the Roif gate",
without result content, until both Roif 2025a/b are cited and the owner
clears the specific publication act. The red-team attack (finding
RT-26, critical) verified that the full results — the verdict in a
heading, the member fractions, the weighted share — sat in this public
repository's ledger, outcome record, phase report, machine record,
claim append, experiment-graph description, and a publication-ready
insert file, with neither the citations nor a recorded clearance. The
gate held on the preprint, the articles, and the website; it was
breached here, in the programme's own register.

Between the two repairs: clearance + citation (option (b)) would spend
the programme's most sensitive external relationship — the Roif credit
and co-authorship question, on which an olive-branch letter of
2026-10-22 stands unanswered — as a retroactive cure for a record
defect, and would publish the results under conditions the owner has
not chosen on their merits. Redaction (option (a)) restores the gate
exactly as frozen, costs the programme nothing it was entitled to
publish, and leaves the (b) route fully available for a future,
deliberate act if Roif replies or the owner later decides otherwise.
Under the programme-success criterion, (a) is the option that protects
the relationship and the register at once.
