**G2, as it stands.** Two necessary conditions are
declared DERIVED on the derivation audit:

- **Condition 1 — the non-certification condition, at
  full strength.** Every state in the domain is
  projection-equivalent to states differing from it in
  reading assignment and language identification, in
  sign-inventory members no object of the record system
  exercises, and in classification of objects outside the
  surviving record set — so no record of the system, and
  no test computed from the records, distinguishes a state
  from every other member of its class. Derived in 7 steps
  from D2 and the frozen projection; counted form executed
  over the coded-corpus class (0 of 3 derivations take a
  reading assignment as an input, independently
  recounted); falsifier: a coded corpus admitted with a
  derivation that computes its table from glyph streams
  *and* a reading assignment. Its load-bearing premise is
  the frozen G1 document's disposition that assertions
  never determine, and its falsifier refutes through that
  basis — both bounds are labeled in the records, not
  smoothed.
- **Condition 2 — the recorded-profile condition, at
  consistency strength.** Every state consistent with the
  records induces, over each coded corpus's object set,
  that corpus's recorded identity structure (exercised
  sign count, token-multiplicity profile, sequence-length
  profile, up to relabeling) — the counted profiles being
  this domain's own (M77: 1,669 inscriptions / 5,361
  tokens / 64 exercised codes; ICIT: 4,410 / 14,213 /
  713; the CISI coded subset: 179 sides / 1,003 tokens /
  182 codes, all recomputed first-hand at the audit). The
  audit confirmed the derivation and fixed its strength:
  its excluding power is exactly consistency's, made
  explicit, class-level, and countable. It is reported at
  that strength and no stronger.

**What did not derive — part of the outcome, not a
footnote.** Candidate 3 (a necessary condition over the

the chain needs a bridge premise equating certification
with a completed validation record, and neither D1–D3 nor
the frozen G1 document supplies it — the G1 document
places validation in the assertion layer, and its
cannot-distinguish account marks the item a statement

The statement's truth-maker would be the register's
bookkeeping at a date, not the states it quantifies over;
its derivable core is already Condition 1's reading
clause. **This marks a real limit of the skeleton's reach
in this domain: register certification is bookkeeping,
not a projection-class fact.** Candidate 4 (standalone
corpus regularities promoted to necessary conditions) is
likewise NOT DERIVED as stated — enumeration of the
observed corpus is the only route to them; they stand as

## Improvement-loop note — proposals for the registry, not acts

Stated as proposals for the VEXA program to dispose of;
this study enacts none of them.

1. **Name the bookkeeping species.** Candidate 3's refusal
   suggests a clarification the skeleton may want: D4
   necessary conditions range over states in
   projection-equivalence classes, while statements whose
   truth-maker is the record system's own bookkeeping
   state (validation statuses, register stages) are a
   distinct species — ledger-state reports — that no
   derivation from D1–D3 will ever promote. Naming the
   boundary in the skeleton would save a future domain
   team the derivation attempt that discovered it here.
2. **Grade derived conditions by strength.** Candidate 2
   met D4's form at consistency strength while Condition
   1 met it at full strength; the distinction carried the
   honest reporting of this study. A strength annotation
   in the G2 outcome vocabulary (full / consistency) would
   let the registry hold both without flattening them.
3. **D3's none-needed branch now has a worked instance.**
   The physics calibration case takes exactly one absolute;
   this domain takes none, by audit, sustained under
   adversarial attack. The branch exists in D3's text; the
   registry may want the instance on record as its first
   exercise.
4. **A falsifier shape worth citing.** Condition 1's
   falsifier refutes the derivation's *application* by
   enlarging the record system past the frozen projection
   (D4's own phrase) — the strongest falsifier available
   for a condition whose content is the projection's
   factoring. Recorded as a worked example; no skeleton
   change proposed on its account.
