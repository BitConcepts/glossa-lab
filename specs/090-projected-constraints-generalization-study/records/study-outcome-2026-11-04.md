## 4. G3–G5 sequencing note (assessment, not a plan)

G3–G5 are commissioned in principle by the same owner
order that commissioned this study; they are **not
started**, and nothing in this section starts them. What
this study's outcome implies for them, as assessment:

- **Order.** The commission's sequence stands — G3, then
  G4, then G5 — and nothing this study found reorders it.
  G1's frozen document and G2's two conditions are the
  substrate each remaining bar is stated against.
- **G3 (one computed separator, validated on held-out
  records) is the natural next bar and the hardest of the
  three.** Its shape is now precise: a separator must be
  computable over records, invariant inside each
  projection-equivalence class, and different across the
  classes it claims to separate (D5). Condition 2's
  counted form is a template for a separator's
  administration (compute a named state's restricted
  table; count deviations from the recorded profile) —
  but it is **not** a separator: for every consistent
  state its deviation count is zero by construction, so
  it separates nothing among consistent states. A G3
  attempt must find a quantity that actually varies
  across classes and validate it on records the
  derivation did not use. Candidate 3's refusal is its
  standing warning: quantities computed over register
  bookkeeping vary with the bookkeeping, not across
  π-classes, and will not be separators.
- **G4 (one instance of the domain's gate refusing or
  killing a claim, from the domain's own records) is a
  framing act more than a machinery act.** The standing
  gate refuses publication while any leg fails, and its
  recorded operation under Specs 086–088 is program
  history a G4 stage may cite as history; the bar asks
  for one instance staged and judged under the skeleton
  framing. Candidate 3's limit applies directly: the
  instance should be a gate refusal over claim content,
  not over register certification, since the latter is
  bookkeeping and would not evidence S5 in the skeleton's
  sense.
- **G5 (reproduction materials) is a materials discipline
  this study has largely pre-paid.** The reproducer's
  package is, in substance: the skeleton, the frozen G1
  document identified by its hash, the derivation and
  audit records, and the repository's records they cite —
  all committed, all hash- or PR-identified. What G5 adds
  is the independent reader: reproduction by a party that
  did not produce the study, under the protocol. Its
  difficulty is organizational, not documentary.
