Hypothesis comparison

Language hypotheses and rival readings compared on the evidence of record, with standings in the outcome vocabulary of record, including NOT IDENTIFIABLE. Comparison never manufactures a winner the adjudications do not name.

Language hypotheses

  • Each hypothesis (including the nonlinguistic position, carried in the same form) with its basis, its model scores where the record holds them, and its standing.
  • Score displays carry the reporting standard: effect size and interval, never a bare p-value.
sources: glossa-indus/hypotheses/ (models, model_claims, model_predictions, model_scores); spec record

Interactions

  • Pick a sign: all readings for it, all rivals, all standings, one table.
  • Pick two hypotheses: their differing predictions listed as predictions, each checkable against the corpus in the explorer.

This surface refuses to

  • Rank readings by agreement with other hypotheses derived from the same evidence (same-lineage agreement is not support).
  • Hide a NOT IDENTIFIABLE outcome behind a confidence percentage.
  • Present a translation for any sequence; the program holds none, and this surface says so where a translation would go.
  • Let a reading's display strength exceed the standing of the weakest reading it rests on.
