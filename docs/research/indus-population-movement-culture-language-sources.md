# Consolidated source notes — read 2026-10-10/11 (index unless noted)
All paraphrased; no long quotations retained.

- Narasimhan et al. 2019 Science 365 eaat7487 DOI 10.1126/science.aat7487 — 523 ancient individuals S/C Asia; Indus Periphery cline (11 outliers Gonur/Shahr-i Sokhta + context), no steppe pre-2000 BCE; Swat later individuals carry Central Steppe MLBA; ANI/ASI formation 2nd mill BCE. Summary via Harvard/Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2019-09-largest-ever-ancient-dna-illuminates-millennia-south.html (index)
- Shinde et al. 2019 Cell 179 DOI 10.1016/j.cell.2019.08.048 — Rakhigarhi I6113, 61 samples screened, >100/109 libraries, low coverage (~31k-86k SNPs depending filter), no steppe / no Iranian-farmer (post-6000 BCE Anatolian-admixed) ancestry; Iranian-related split earlier. URL https://www.cell.com/cell/fulltext/S0092-8674(19)30967-5 (index) + commentary https://www.cell.com/cell/fulltext/S0092-8674(19)31078-5
- Valentine et al. 2015 PLoS ONE 10:e0123103 DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0123103 — Sr/Pb enamel Harappa R-37 + Farmana, cemeteries almost entirely first-generation immigrants, childhood hinterland shift; only ~600 Indus skeletons known total; cemeteries selective, not population cross-section. VERIFIED LIVE via browser_open journals.plos.org printable version. URL https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article/file?type=printable&id=10.1371/journal.pone.0123103
- Mutin et al. 2025 Sci Rep DOI 10.1038/s41598-025-92621-5 — 23 Mehrgarh Neolithic tooth-enamel 14C, Bayesian: Period I cemetery 5223-4914 to 4769-4679 BCE, 186-531 yr, vs Jarrige ~8000 BCE charcoal chronology; authors infer later diffusion from west, note multidirectional/local adaptation possible. Via Nature India https://www.nature.com/articles/d44151-025-00060-y and HAL https://hal.science/hal-05036385v1/preview/s41598-025-92621-5.pdf (index) — CONTESTED / not yet consensus.
- Kenoyer 1991 Indus Valley Tradition PDF https://www.harappa.com/sites/default/files/pdf/Kenoyer1991%20Indus%20Valley%20Tradition.pdf — regional interaction, Mehrgarh dental Asian affinities (Lukacs), local + western domesticates.
- Lukacs & Hemphill via Mehrgarh summary: dental biodistance — cultural continuity Neolithic-Chalcolithic Mehrgarh but population discontinuity / moderate gene flow; Neolithic Mehrgarh closer to Chalcolithic Inamgaon than to Chalcolithic Mehrgarh. Secondary summary https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mehrgarh (index, flag as secondary)
- Meluhha: Possehl compilation via IIC PDF https://aws-static.iicdelhi.in/s3fs-public/2020-11/145615June132013_IIC_48_13_06_2013.pdf — 76 citations, goods lists, Shu-ilishu interpreter seal; Indus seals in Ur/Kish/Babylon/Susa (secondary syntheses).
- Elamo-Dravidian: McAlpin 1981; critiques Krishnamurti, Zvelebil, Starostin; Pedron 2023 reassessment insufficient. Summary https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elamo-Dravidian_languages (index, secondary but cites primary critiques)
- Brahui: North Dravidian isolate in Baluchistan, >1500 km from nearest Dravidian, heavy Balochi loans, only ~15% of ethnic Brahui primary speakers (Elfenbein via summary). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brahvi_language ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Dravidian_languages
- Witzel substrate: ~300 Rigvedic loans, earliest layer Para-Munda / Kubha-Vipash substrate (not Dravidian), later Dravidian contact; retroflex areal; critics Thieme, Das — no consensus per-word. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Substratum_in_Vedic_Sanskrit
- Cemetery H / PGW / Gandhara Grave: candidates not proofs for Indo-Aryan association; Kennedy/Kenoyer — Cemetery H biological affinity to earlier Harappa, may be settlement reorganisation not invasion. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cemetery_H_culture ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Painted_Grey_Ware_culture ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gandhara_grave_culture ; Swat admixture with IAMC steppe-carrying population dated 1900-1500 BCE (modelled, burials c.1200-800 BCE).
- Moorjani et al. 2013 AJHG DOI 10.1016/j.ajhg.2013.07.006 — modern DNA LD dating ANI-ASI mixture 1900-4200 yr ago, 73 groups; indirect, model-dependent. https://repository.ias.ac.in/107395/
- Farmer/Sproat/Witzel 2004 non-linguistic thesis vs Rao et al. 2009 Science conditional entropy — neither proves language / family. https://www.newscientist.com/article/1934175-scholars-at-odds-over-mysterious-indus-script/
- Mahadevan M77: 2906 texts, 417 signs, avg 5 signs; CISI larger. https://www.harappa.com/blog/indus-concordance-and-tamil-potsherds-now-online ; PLoS n-gram paper https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0009506 (EBUDS exclusions noted)
- Zebu: independent indicine domestication Indus/Mehrgarh, mtDNA I1/I2, Chen et al 2010 etc via https://www.nature.com/articles/hdy201030
- Datasets: AADR Harvard Dataverse DOI 10.7910/DVN/FFIDCW https://reich.hms.harvard.edu/sites/reich.hms.harvard.edu/files/inline-files/AADR%20bioRxiv.pdf ; Poseidon https://elifesciences.org/reviewed-preprints/98317v1/pdf + server.poseidon-adna.org; XRONOS https://xronos.ch/ + https://journal.caa-international.org/articles/191/files/69034153b73bf.pdf (>350k dates, 75k sites, Roe et al 2025); c14bazAAR/RADON via https://github.com/isaakiel/r_tutorial_caa_2018/blob/HEAD/scripts/second_part_c14bazAAR.md ; IsoArcH https://hal.science/hal-03677852v1/file/Salesse_et%20al_2018.pdf + https://doi.isoarch.org/10.48530/isoarch.2021.016 (note: core coverage Graeco-Roman/Mediterranean, sparse for Indus — use as schema, supplement with primary SI); Glottolog CLDF CC-BY 4.0 https://github.com/glottolog/glottolog-cldf/blob/HEAD/cldf/README.md ; D-PLACE CC / Zenodo v3 https://zenodo.org/records/10453887 ; IE-CoR https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-025-05445-3 ; DEDR via DSAL / SARVA http://kolichala.com/DEDR/ (copyright restricted — flag); TwoRains supplementary Zenodo https://zenodo.org/records/3909832 ; Petrie/Bates archaeobotany Masudpur https://api.repository.cam.ac.uk/server/api/core/bitstreams/cba1587e-f742-4308-baad-331fc385b8db/content ; Rice Archaeobotanical Database RAD via Fuller et al summary in ResearchGate fig caption (flag access variable).
