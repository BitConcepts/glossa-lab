## Ask 1 — D8 audit scope and order → **1B**

The D8 source/licence audit runs **sequenced, with a
stop rule and a reported outcome after each stage**:
**Law (2011) first, Kenoyer/Vidale second, Meluhha
compilations last**. Law (2011) is first because it is
the largest structured provenance dataset in D8 and
its rights outcome may decide Q7's viability on its
own; a rights failure at any stage is a reportable
negative closure for the affected response arm, not a
prompt to substitute a weaker source. The audit is a
desk audit of published sources and their terms; it
involves **no external contact of any kind**, and no
digitisation before the audit's rights outcome for
that component.

## Ask 2 — What counts as a provenance link → **2B**

**Two-tier links.** Instrumental methods
(petrography, INAA, LA-ICP-MS, Pb isotopes) form the
**primary link set**; visual-only links appear only in
a **separately labelled sensitivity**. The primary
estimand is defined on strong links: if the D8 audit
shows strong links too sparse for the Ask 5
minimum-support rule, Q7 is NOT-YET-TESTABLE on the
primary — it is not quietly re-based on visual links.

## Ask 3 — Response operationalisation → **3A**

**Each response is modelled on its own eligible site
set**: ceramic similarity from published
fabric/typology classifications; bead/craft similarity
from published technique classifications; a site
enters an arm if that arm's frozen minimum-support
rule is met; the arms are never pooled into one
score (§8.1). Minimum-support and frequency rules are
fixed in the Q7 freeze **from margins only**, with
provenance method and classification source recorded
per link and per similarity cell. The two arms may
return different verdicts; that is a feature of the
bounded question.

## Ask 4 — Dependence on Spec 030 → **4A**

**Q7 proceeds independently of the Spec 030
chronology-spine extension**, on the Stage 0
gazetteer and published period labels
(`period_label_as_published`). Q7's blocker is the D8
audit, not chronology. What stays untestable under 4A
is recorded with the decision: any Q7 variant needing
within-Mature chronological resolution finer than
published period labels would be a new question with
its own design, not a reason to hold this one.

## Ask 5 — Null model, controls, support rules → **5A**

The Spec 026/027 discipline is inherited **wholesale**:
MRM / partial-Mantel null (geography and river
distance explain all shared variance); the §8.1
falsifier (source-sharing adds no predictive power,
or its effect is not robust to the frozen site
jackknife); discriminating positive and negative
controls (a control that cannot differentially fail
makes a run INVALID); the INVALID / INCONCLUSIVE /
CONTRADICTED / SUPPORTED verdict taxonomy. The
minimum eligible site-pair and per-arm support rules
are fixed in the Q7 freeze **from margins only, after
the D8 audit reports the eligible-link counts and
before any response is computed**.

## Ask 6 — Publication posture and registration → **6A**

are published only at their audited posture

recorded under Spec 027 and never written into Stage
