Primary action

Changed & why
Reports today are a file browser (name/kind/size/updated) with no relationship to the claims they report on, and the ledger — the constitution's sole continuity authority — has no surface at all. Here reports carry their publication state honestly (including “prepared, awaiting approval”), and the ledger is readable in the tool that writes it, append-only by construction.
