# Battery case (ii) — a planted unfalsifiable headline claim

**Artifact:** `claims.json` in this directory — a planted register,
written for this battery. It is not a programme spec.

**The defect, in plain words.** The headline claim (FB2-C01) has
a falsifier in form and none in substance. Its stated test —
"defeated if the signary is shown to lack the internal coherence
that the calendrical reading reveals" — can never be run: the
only judge of "coherence" the claim admits is the reading
itself, so any sequence that fits is evidence for the claim and
no sequence can count against it. A claim nothing could defeat
is not a hypothesis; it is a label. The register's second claim
(FB2-C02) is deliberately sound — genuinely falsifiable, and
true — so the battery tests whether the evaluator finds the
rotten claim in a register that is not rotten throughout.

**What the battery asks of the evaluator.** If the evaluator
raises a correctness finding against FB2-C01 (a falsifiability,
irreducibility, or equivalent challenge that names the empty
test), the gate catches this case. If FB2-C01 passes with only
draft-stage hygiene findings, that is recorded as a named blind
spot in the battery results: as operated, the evaluator does
not detect a falsifier that is empty in substance.
