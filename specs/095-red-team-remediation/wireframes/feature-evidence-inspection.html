Evidence inspection

The claim-to-source walk (protocol steps 3 and 4): pick a claim, see its falsifier and its evidence, and walk each evidence item's lineage back to its named source. Also the audit-trail export surface (protocol step 8).

Claim picker (left rail)

  • Ledger search and filters: standing, study, sign, falsifier present or absent (the 9 falsifier-less claims are findable as a class; the gap is shown, not hidden).
  • Claim detail header: statement, falsifier verbatim, does-not-establish bound, stage and status, supersession chain.
source: glossa-indus/ledger/claims.jsonl via the ledger gate's own validation

Lineage walk (primary)

claim → evidence item → derivation step → derivation step → named source
  • Each evidence item shows its lineage type and independence class in the standards vocabulary; a derived item is labelled derived wherever it appears.
  • Matrix drill-down: the claim's row in the evidence matrix, cell by cell, each cell resolving to its item.
  • What the evidence does not establish is printed on the item, next to what it does.
sources: claims evidence_refs; glossa-indus/matrix/ (994 × 644); STANDARDS.md lineage vocabulary

Export the audit trail (protocol step 8)

  • From any claim or candidate: export the full trail (claim, falsifier, evidence lineage, adjudications) as a file the researcher keeps. The export is the records themselves, not a summary written for export.

This surface refuses to

  • Show an evidence item without its lineage, or an independence claim stronger than its typing carries.
  • Count a derived result as independent confirmation of its own source.
  • Export a summary in place of the underlying records.
