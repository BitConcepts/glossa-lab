# Per-reading falsifiers — the 94 strict-core Indus readings (annex to the Spec 091 standings record)

Filed 2026-11-21 under the owner's order of that day (Glossa-Lab chat,
Workstream D). This annex closes the gap recorded during preparation of
the Co-scientist audit package for the 94 strict readings: the filed
audit recorded no per-reading falsifier for any of the 94 readings.

Each falsifier below is drafted from that reading's own filed claim and
evidence basis, as recorded in the filed anchor-support audit of the
strict core and its canonical anchor register, updated by the two later
corrected revalidation rounds (the same sources as the audit package
appendix of 2026-11-20). Each names the sign, the reading, the evidence
line it stands on, and the concrete observation, count, or test result
that would defeat or demote that reading.

This annex adds falsifiers only. It changes no standing, re-adjudicates
no reading, and alters no other record. Standings are stated as they
stand at filing: 6 independently supported / 65 supported on internal
evidence only / 4 requires revalidation (M087, M165, M264, M311) /
3 expert-pending (M222, M362, M398) / 16 insufficient evidence.

Coverage: 93 of the 94 readings carry a specific falsifier below.
1 reading is flagged as filed basis too thin to support a specific
falsifier: M099 (kol/koḷ) — the flag and what is missing are stated on
its row rather than an invented falsifier.
